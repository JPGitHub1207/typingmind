# Further Education Training Provider Networks in London

This document lists the major further education (FE) training provider networks operating in London.

## Major FE College Groups/Networks

### 1. **AoC London (Association of Colleges London)**
- **Description**: Regional branch of the Association of Colleges representing FE colleges across London
- **Website**: https://www.aoc.co.uk/about-us/aoc-regions/aoc-london
- **Members**: All FE colleges in London
- **Role**: Advocacy, support, and representation for London FE colleges

### 2. **Capital City Colleges Group**
- **Description**: One of London's largest FE college groups
- **Colleges**: 
  - City and Islington College
  - Westminster Kingsway College
  - The College of Haringey, Enfield and North East London (CONEL)
- **Website**: https://www.capitalccg.ac.uk/

### 3. **New City College**
- **Description**: Large FE college group in East London
- **Colleges**:
  - Hackney Community College
  - Redbridge College
  - Tower Hamlets College
  - Epping Forest College
  - Havering College of Further and Higher Education
- **Website**: https://www.ncclondon.ac.uk/

### 4. **London South East Colleges**
- **Description**: FE college group serving South East London
- **Colleges**:
  - Bromley College
  - Bexley College
  - Greenwich Community College
- **Website**: https://www.lsec.ac.uk/

### 5. **United Colleges Group**
- **Description**: FE college group in West London
- **Colleges**:
  - City of Westminster College
  - College of North West London
- **Website**: https://www.ucg.ac.uk/

### 6. **Waltham Forest College**
- **Description**: Standalone FE college in North East London
- **Website**: https://www.waltham.ac.uk/

### 7. **Lewisham Southwark College**
- **Description**: FE college group in South London
- **Website**: https://www.lewisham-southwark.ac.uk/

### 8. **Richmond and Hillcroft Adult Community College**
- **Description**: Adult education college in South West London
- **Website**: https://www.rhacc.ac.uk/

## Sector-Specific Networks

### 9. **Film London - Metro London Skills Cluster**
- **Description**: Skills development network for the film and media industry
- **Partners**: Multiple FE colleges and training providers
- **Website**: https://www.aoc.co.uk/corporate-services/projects/film-london-metro-london-skills-cluster

### 10. **Creating a Greener London – Sustainable Construction Skills**
- **Description**: Network focused on sustainable construction skills training
- **Partners**: FE colleges and construction industry partners
- **Website**: https://www.aoc.co.uk/corporate-services/projects/creating-a-greener-london-sustainable-construction-skills

## Independent Training Provider Networks

### 11. **London Training Providers Network**
- **Description**: Network of independent training providers in London. Part of AELP's regional network structure.
- **Website**: https://www.aelp.org.uk/membership/regional-network-membership/
- **Parent Organization**: AELP (Association of Employment and Learning Providers) - https://www.aelp.org.uk
- **Focus**: Apprenticeships, adult education, and skills training

### 12. **London Apprenticeship Provider Network**
- **Description**: Network of apprenticeship training providers. Part of AELP's regional network structure.
- **Website**: https://www.aelp.org.uk/membership/regional-network-membership/
- **Parent Organization**: AELP (Association of Employment and Learning Providers) - https://www.aelp.org.uk
- **Focus**: Apprenticeship delivery and quality

## Specialist Networks

### 13. **London Adult Learning Providers Network**
- **Description**: Network of adult education providers. May operate through Learning and Work Institute or local authority networks.
- **Website**: https://www.learningandwork.org.uk
- **Alternative Resource**: https://www.london.gov.uk/what-we-do/education-and-youth/skills-and-employment
- **Focus**: Adult and community learning

### 14. **London Sixth Form Colleges Network**
- **Description**: Network of sixth form colleges in London
- **Focus**: A-level and vocational qualifications for 16-19 year olds

## Key Organizations Supporting FE Networks

### 15. **Greater London Authority (GLA) - Skills and Employment**
- **Description**: London-wide skills and employment strategy
- **Website**: https://www.london.gov.uk/what-we-do/education-and-youth/skills-and-employment
- **Role**: Strategic oversight and funding for skills training

### 16. **Education and Skills Funding Agency (ESFA)**
- **Description**: Government agency funding FE providers
- **Website**: https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/education-and-skills-funding-agency
- **Role**: Funding and regulation of FE providers

### 17. **Ofsted**
- **Description**: Inspectorate for FE providers
- **Website**: https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ofsted
- **Role**: Quality assurance and inspection

## Additional Resources

- **Find a Learning Provider**: https://www.gov.uk/check-a-learning-provider
- **London Colleges Directory**: https://www.aoc.co.uk/about-colleges/college-directory/
- **Skills for Londoners**: GLA initiative for skills development

## Notes

- This list includes both formal networks (college groups) and informal networks (sector-specific collaborations)
- Many colleges are part of multiple networks
- Network structures and memberships may change over time
- Some networks are formal legal entities (college groups), while others are collaborative partnerships
