# Complete UK Funded Training Providers - Ofsted Data

This document contains information about ALL 2458 Funded Training Providers scraped from the Ofsted website.

## Data Source
- **Website**: https://reports.ofsted.gov.uk/search?q=&location=&lat=&lon=&radius=&level_1_types=1&level_2_types%5B%5D=3
- **Date Scraped**: October 20, 2025
- **Total Providers**: 2458

## Category Breakdown
- **Independent learning provider**: 1377 providers (56.0%)
- **Training provider**: 467 providers (19.0%)
- ****: 330 providers (13.4%)
- **Further education college**: 183 providers (7.4%)
- **Apprenticeship provider**: 101 providers (4.1%)
