## Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Slow Follower Growth
**Mitigation:**
- Increase posting frequency to 2x/day
- Focus 100% on Reels (highest reach)
- Join 3+ engagement pods
- Collaborate with 5+ micro-influencers
- Run $50-100 Instagram ads to test

### Risk 2: Low Engagement
**Mitigation:**
- A/B test caption styles
- More question-based CTAs
- Respond to every comment within 1 hour
- Create more controversy/hot takes
- Use trending audio and hashtags

### Risk 3: No Monetization After 90 Days
**Mitigation:**
- Focus entirely on affiliate (lowest barrier)
- Create irresistible digital product ($7-15 range)
- Reach out to 50+ brands, not just 30
- Offer discounted first-time collaborations
- Create sponsored content examples (to show brands)

### Risk 4: Platform Bans/Restrictions
**Mitigation:**
- Always disclose AI nature in bio
- Build email list from day 1
- Multi-platform presence (3+ platforms)
- Own your website and content
- Follow all platform guidelines strictly

### Risk 5: Tool Costs Too High
**Mitigation:**
- Use minimal budget version ($283/month)
- Use free tiers aggressively
- Build more with Cursor to replace paid tools
- Midjourney batch generation (generate 200+ then pause)
- Use Stable Diffusion locally (free)
