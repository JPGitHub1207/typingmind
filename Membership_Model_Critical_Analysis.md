# Critical Analysis: Tiered Membership Model with Training Upsells

## Your Proposed Model

### Entry Membership: £100/month (£1,200/year)
- 1 full day event each quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups
- 20% off training (2 half days per quarter)

### Member: £280/month (£3,360/year)
- 2 places
- Rising star network (shadow, supported)
- All training included (free)
- 1 full day event
- Monthly meetups
- 2 online half days each month

### Growth: £420/month (£5,040/year)
- 3 places
- All above
- All training free
- 6 online half days (unlimited)
- What else?

**Upsell:** Training courses

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES

### 1. PRICING IS TOO LOW - You're Undervaluing Yourself

**The Problem:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**Reality Check:**
- You're targeting large employers (corporate budgets)
- They pay £5,000-10,000+ for consulting
- Your prices are consumer-level, not enterprise-level
- You're leaving money on the table

**Comparison:**
- CIPD membership: £120-200/year (but that's for individuals)
- Professional networks: £2,000-10,000/year (for corporates)
- Your prices: £1,200-5,040/year (too low for target market)

**Recommendation:**
- Entry: £200-300/month (£2,400-3,600/year)
- Member: £500-750/month (£6,000-9,000/year)
- Growth: £1,000-1,500/month (£12,000-18,000/year)

**Why:** Corporate budgets can handle this. You're targeting wrong price point.

---

### 2. "CHEAP AND UPSELL" IS DANGEROUS - Creates Wrong Perception

**The Problem:**
- "Cheap" = low value perception
- Upsell = feels like bait-and-switch
- Customers feel tricked
- Damages trust

**Better Approach:**
- Price appropriately from start
- Training included in higher tiers (not upsell)
- Value-based pricing, not "cheap + upsell"

**Why This Matters:**
- Corporate buyers are smart
- They see through "cheap + upsell"
- Prefer transparent, value-based pricing
- "Cheap" attracts wrong customers

---

### 3. MEMBERSHIP TIERS ARE CONFUSING - Too Many Variables

**The Problem:**
- Entry: 1 full day, monthly meetups, 20% off training
- Member: 2 places, rising star, all training free, 1 full day, monthly, 2 online half days
- Growth: 3 places, all above, 6 online half days

**Issues:**
- Too many different benefits
- Hard to compare
- Unclear value proposition
- Confusing for customers

**Better Approach:**
- Simplify tiers
- Clear value progression
- Easy to understand
- "More of the same" not "different things"

**Example:**
- **Starter:** 1 person, 4 events/year, monthly meetups
- **Professional:** 2 people, 4 events/year, monthly meetups, training included
- **Enterprise:** 3+ people, 4 events/year, monthly meetups, all training, priority support

---

### 4. "RISING STAR NETWORK" IS VAGUE - What Does This Mean?

**The Problem:**
- "Rising star network (shadow, supported)"
- Unclear what this is
- Hard to explain
- Hard to deliver
- Hard to value

**Questions:**
- What is "shadow"?
- What is "supported"?
- Who are "rising stars"?
- How do you deliver this?
- What's the value?

**Better Approach:**
- Be specific
- Clear deliverables
- Measurable value
- Easy to explain

**Example:**
- "Mentoring program: Pair junior staff with senior leaders"
- "Career development: Quarterly 1-on-1 sessions"
- "Leadership development: Access to executive coaching"

---

### 5. TRAINING AS UPSELL VS. INCLUDED - Creates Confusion

**The Problem:**
- Entry: 20% off training (upsell)
- Member: All training included (free)
- Growth: All training free

**Issues:**
- Entry tier feels like "bait" (cheap but pay for training)
- Creates two revenue models (membership + training)
- Confusing pricing
- Hard to explain

**Better Approach:**
- **Option A:** Training included in all tiers (different amounts)
- **Option B:** Training is separate product (not membership benefit)
- **Not Both:** Don't mix membership + upsell training

**Recommendation:**
- Training included in higher tiers
- Or training is separate business
- Not "membership + upsell training"

---

### 6. "WHAT ELSE?" FOR GROWTH TIER - You Don't Know What You're Selling

**The Problem:**
- "What else?" = you haven't thought through value
- Adding random benefits = not strategic
- Customers see through this
- Hard to justify price

**Better Approach:**
- Know what you're selling
- Clear value proposition
- Strategic benefits
- Justified pricing

**What Could Growth Tier Include:**
- Priority access to events
- Exclusive content/resources
- 1-on-1 consulting sessions
- Custom training programs
- Dedicated account manager
- Early access to new products
- Thought leadership opportunities

**But:** Only if it adds real value, not just "what else?"

---

### 7. COST STRUCTURE NOT CONSIDERED - Can You Deliver This?

**The Problem:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- But: 4 full-day events + 12 monthly meetups = 16 events/year
- Cost per event: £3,000-10,000 (venue, catering, your time)
- 16 events × £5,000 = £80,000 costs
- Need 67 Entry members just to break even on events
- Plus your time, overhead, etc.

**Reality Check:**
- Entry tier may not be profitable
- Need to calculate unit economics
- Can you deliver at this price?
- What's your break-even?

**Recommendation:**
- Calculate costs per event
- Calculate costs per member
- Calculate break-even
- Price accordingly
