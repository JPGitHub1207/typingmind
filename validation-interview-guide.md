# Event Validation Interview Guide
**50 Conversations Before You Spend a Euro**

---

## PURPOSE

Before investing time and money in your event, you must validate:
1. **Problem existence:** Do people have the pain you're solving?
2. **Solution fit:** Would your event actually solve it?
3. **Willingness to pay:** Will they buy tickets?
4. **Competitive positioning:** Why choose you over alternatives?

**Target: 50 interviews across 5 segments (10 each)**

---

## INTERVIEW LOGISTICS

### Who to Interview

**Segment 1: Established Space Companies** (10 interviews)
- Target: VP/Director level at primes, system integrators
- Examples: Airbus DS, Thales, OHB, satellite operators
- Key question: What brings them to events? (partnerships, talent, market intel)

**Segment 2: Space Startups** (10 interviews)
- Target: CEO or Head of Business Development
- Stage: Seed to Series B
- Key question: What's the ROI they need? (fundraising, customers, PR)

**Segment 3: Investors** (10 interviews)
- Target: Partners at VC/PE funds active in space
- Examples: Seraphim, E2MC, Space Angels, Techstars, corporate VCs
- Key question: What makes them attend? (deal flow, market intelligence)

**Segment 4: Government/Agencies** (10 interviews)
- Target: Program managers, business development
- Examples: ESA, national agencies, EU commission
- Key question: What gets budget approval? (policy influence, ecosystem building)

**Segment 5: Adjacent Tech/Services** (10 interviews)
- Target: Companies adjacent to space (AI/ML, cloud, consulting)
- Examples: AWS, Google Cloud, Deloitte, cybersecurity firms
- Key question: Why would they care about space? (new markets, partnerships)

**LinkedIn Message (Cold):**

```
at these events.

I'm specifically interested in understanding what works and what doesn't 
from an attendee perspective. This is pure research - not selling anything.

Would you be open to a quick call this week?

Best,
[Your name]
```

**Email (Warm Intro):**
```
Subject: Quick input on space tech events?

Hi [Name],

[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out. I'm doing research on the 
European space tech event market and would value your input.

I'm interviewing 50 people across the ecosystem to understand:
- What events you currently attend and why
- What's missing or frustrating about existing options
- What would make a new event valuable

15-20 minutes, your choice of time. Happy to share findings with you 
after I compile all the insights.

Interested?

[Your name]
```

**Success Tips:**
- ✅ Make it about THEM, not your idea (research, not pitching)
- ✅ Keep time commitment low (15-20 min)
- ✅ Offer value exchange (share insights after)
- ✅ Use warm intros when possible (LinkedIn 2nd connections)
- ❌ Don't pitch your event idea yet (too early, biases responses)

## INTERVIEW SCRIPT

### Opening (2 minutes)

"Thanks for taking the time. As I mentioned, I'm doing research on the European 
space tech event landscape. I'm interviewing about 50 people to understand what's 
working and what's not.

This is pure research - I'm not selling anything today. I just want to understand 
your perspective. Everything you share will be confidential.

Sound good? Great, let's jump in."

---

### SECTION 1: Current Behavior (5 minutes)

**Q1: "What space-related events have you attended in the past 2 years?"**

*Listen for:*
- Which specific events
- How many events per year
- Geographic spread (local vs international)

*Follow-up:* "Of those, which were most valuable? Why?"

---

**Q2: "Walk me through why you decided to attend [specific event they mentioned]?"**

*Listen for:*
- Who made the decision (them vs company policy)
- What outcomes they hoped for
- What alternatives they considered

*Follow-up:* "What was the actual ROI for you? Meet the goal?"

---

**Q3: "What events have you stopped attending? Why?"**

*Listen for:*
- Specific pain points (this is gold!)
- Changed priorities
- Budget constraints
- Better alternatives found

*Follow-up:* "What would have made you continue attending?"

---

### SECTION 2: Pain Points & Frustrations (5 minutes)

**Q4: "When you think about space tech events in Europe, what frustrates you most?"**

*Listen for:*
- Content quality issues
- Networking challenges
- Cost/ROI concerns
- Format problems
- Logistics issues

*Follow-up:* "Has that caused you to skip events or change behavior?"

---

**Q5: "If you could fix ONE thing about European space tech events, what would it be?"**

*Listen for:*
- The one pain point that rises above others
- Whether it's content, networking, format, cost, or something else

*Follow-up:* "Have you seen any event that does this well?"

---

**Q6: "What types of people do you WANT to meet at events but struggle to connect with?"**

*Listen for:*
- Specific roles (VCs, CTOs, customers)
- Why current networking isn't working
- Ideal introduction scenarios

---

### SECTION 3: Ideal Event (Concept Testing) (5 minutes)

**Q7: "Imagine an ideal space tech event - what would it look like?"**

*Listen for:*
- Format preferences
- Content topics
- Networking structure
- Size, location, timing
- Duration

*Don't interrupt - let them dream*

---

**Q8: "What would make an event a 'must-attend' versus 'nice-to-have' for you?"**

*Listen for:*
- Critical success factors
- What triggers "I can't miss this"
- FOMO drivers

*Follow-up:* "Give me an example of a 'must-attend' event (any industry)."

---

**NOW Introduce Your Concept (If time permits):**

"Based on my research so far, I'm seeing a gap in [X]. I'm considering an event 
that focuses on [specific positioning - be specific!]. 

It would be [format description] for [target audience] to achieve [outcome].

Reactions?"

*Listen for:*
- Genuine interest vs polite enthusiasm
- Specific concerns or objections
- Suggestions for improvement
- Who else they'd expect to attend

---

### SECTION 4: Willingness to Pay (3 minutes)

**Q9: "What's the typical event budget you have per year?"**

*Listen for:*
- Total budget available
- How it's allocated
- Who controls it (them vs manager)

---

**Q10: "For an event like what I just described, what would you expect to pay?"**

*Listen for:*
- Price range they state
- Caveats ("depends on X")
- Comparison to current events

*Follow-up:* "At what price would you definitely say yes? At what price would it 
be too expensive?"

**Key Metric: Van Westendorp Pricing**
- Too cheap (seems low quality): €___
- Bargain (great value): €___
- Expensive but I'd consider: €___
- Too expensive (never pay): €___

---

**Q11: "If this event were happening in [month/location], would you attend?"**

*Listen for:*
- Enthusiasm level (1-10 scale)
- Immediate objections
- Conditional yes ("if X, then yes")

*Follow-up if yes:* "What would prevent you from actually showing up after 
registering?"

---

### SECTION 5: Validation & Referrals (2 minutes)

**Q12: "Who else should I talk to? Who would be most interested in this type of event?"**

*Listen for:*
- Referrals (warm intros if possible)
- Other segments you hadn't considered

---

**Q13: "If I launch this, would you be open to being on an advisory board or 
providing feedback as I develop it?"**

*Listen for:*
- Level of commitment
- Conditions for involvement

---

### Closing

"This has been super helpful. As I mentioned, I'm doing about 50 of these 
conversations and will compile insights. Happy to share findings with you 
when I'm done.

Two quick admin things:

1. Can I follow up with you as the concept develops? (Get email/LinkedIn)
2. Anyone else you think I should talk to? (Get referrals)

Thanks again!"

## POST-INTERVIEW SCORING

**Immediately after each interview, rate:**

### Problem Severity (1-5)
- 5 = Acute pain, actively seeking solution
- 4 = Significant pain, would pay to solve
- 3 = Moderate pain, might pay if easy
- 2 = Minor annoyance, wouldn't pay
- 1 = No pain expressed

**Score: ___**

---

### Solution Fit (1-5)
- 5 = "This is exactly what I need"
- 4 = "This would solve my problem"
- 3 = "This might help"
- 2 = "Not sure this solves my problem"
- 1 = "This doesn't address my needs"

**Score: ___**

---

### Willingness to Pay (1-5)
- 5 = "Tell me where to buy tickets"
- 4 = "I'd definitely pay [specific price]"
- 3 = "Maybe, depending on price/details"
- 2 = "Probably not"
- 1 = "No way I'd pay for this"

**Score: ___**

---

### Enthusiasm (1-5)
- 5 = Extremely excited, offered to help
- 4 = Definitely interested, wants updates
- 3 = Moderately interested
- 2 = Polite but not engaged
- 1 = Disinterested or negative

**Score: ___**

---

### Validation Score (Average of 4 scores above)

**Total: ___ / 5**

**Target: Average of 3.5+ across all 50 interviews to proceed**

**Decision:** Continue with next segment or pivot concept?

### After 50 Interviews (Full Validation)

**GO/NO-GO CRITERIA:**

✅ **GREEN LIGHT (Proceed):**
- Average validation score: 3.5+
- 30+ people scored 4+ on "willingness to pay"
- Consistent pain points across segments
- Clear pricing consensus (within €100 range)
- 5+ advisory board commitments
- Less than 3 major objections without mitigation

🟡 **YELLOW LIGHT (Conditional):**
- Average validation score: 3.0-3.5
- 20-30 people interested but with caveats
- Pain points exist but not acute
- Pricing unclear or widely varied
- Major objections raised but addressable
- **Action:** Refine concept, do 10 more interviews

🔴 **RED LIGHT (Stop or Major Pivot):**
- Average validation score: <3.0
- <20 people willing to pay
- Pain points minor or inconsistent
- Strong negative feedback
- Better alternatives exist
- Fundamental objections (can't mitigate)

## COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

### ❌ Confirmation Bias
**Problem:** Only hearing what you want to hear  
**Solution:** 
- Have someone else review interview notes
- Track negative feedback explicitly
- Weight negative feedback 2x (people are polite)

### ❌ Asking Leading Questions
**Problem:** "Wouldn't it be great if there was an event that..."  
**Solution:** 
- Ask about current behavior first
- Let them describe problems before proposing solutions
- Use open-ended questions

### ❌ Talking Too Much
**Problem:** You pitch for 15 minutes, they listen politely  
**Solution:** 
- 80/20 rule: They talk 80%, you talk 20%
- Ask follow-ups: "Tell me more about that"
- Embrace awkward silence (they'll fill it with gold)

### ❌ Only Talking to People Who Like You
**Problem:** Biased sample (friends are supportive)  
**Solution:** 
- Talk to strangers
- Seek out skeptics
- Interview competitors' customers

### ❌ Stopping at 10 Interviews
**Problem:** Not enough data to see patterns  
**Solution:** 
- Commit to 50 before starting
- Spread across all 5 segments
- Keep going even when it feels repetitive

### ❌ Not Testing Price
**Problem:** Assume they'll pay, don't validate  
**Solution:** 
- Always ask pricing questions
- Use Van Westendorp (4 price points)
- Note the enthusiasm drop when price mentioned

### ❌ Treating "Maybe" as "Yes"
**Problem:** Optimistic interpretation of lukewarm interest  
**Solution:** 
- Only count 4-5/5 scores as validation
- "Interesting" ≠ "I'll buy tickets"
- Track enthusiasm drop-off

## TIMELINE FOR VALIDATION

### Week 1: Prep
- [ ] Finalize interview script
- [ ] Create target list (50 people across 5 segments)
- [ ] Draft outreach messages
- [ ] Set up tracking spreadsheet

### Week 2-5: Interviews (12-15 per week)
- [ ] Send outreach messages (batch of 30-40)
- [ ] Conduct 12-15 interviews per week
- [ ] Score and document immediately after each
- [ ] Refine script based on learnings

### Week 6: Analysis
- [ ] Compile all interview data
- [ ] Identify patterns and outliers
- [ ] Calculate validation scores
- [ ] Write validation report

### Week 7: Decision
- [ ] Review report with advisors/partners
- [ ] Make GO/NO-GO decision
- [ ] If GO: Begin Phase 2 (advisory board, venue, etc.)
- [ ] If NO-GO: Consider pivot or alternative approaches

**Total Timeline: 7 weeks** (if you hustle)

## SUCCESS STORY EXAMPLE

### Real Validation Case Study

"I interviewed 52 people across the European space tech ecosystem. Here's what I found:

- **Problem:** 73% said they struggle to connect with investors at current events ('too many people, not enough structure')
- **Solution fit:** My concept of structured 1-on-1 investor matchmaking scored 4.2/5 avg
- **Willingness to pay:** 34 people (65%) said they'd pay €600+ for this
- **Key insight:** Startups would pay MORE than I thought (€800+) if investor quality was guaranteed

**Decision:** Green light to proceed. Key change based on feedback: Shifted from 'open event' to 'application-only' to maintain quality.

**Result:** Year 1: 280 attendees, 65 NPS, 40+ deals in pipeline, 85% 'would attend again'"
