## 🎯 OPPORTUNITY #1: Large Employer Providers Network

### Market Analysis

**Market Size:**
- 200 large employer providers with direct contracts
- Corporate budgets = more money available
- Less staff = need for support
- Some have failed (e.g., Citron - Grade 4) = need help

**Key Characteristics:**
- ✅ **More money** - Corporate budgets, not training provider margins
- ✅ **Less staff** - Not their core business, need support
- ✅ **Higher willingness to pay** - Can charge premium
- ✅ **Real pain points** - Compliance, Ofsted, regulations
- ✅ **Some failures** - Citron example shows they need help

**Pain Points:**
- Managing Ofsted requirements (not their expertise)
- DfE funding compliance
- Awarding body/EPA regulations
- Quality management
- They're corporates, not training experts

### Why This Could Work

1. **Higher Price Point**
   - Corporate budgets vs. training provider margins
   - Can charge £2,000-5,000+ per network (vs. £1,000 for training providers)
   - They're used to paying for expertise

2. **Real Need**
   - They've taken on training provider responsibilities
   - It's not their core business = they need help
   - Failures like Citron show the risk

3. **Less Competition**
   - AELP focuses on traditional training providers
   - Large employers are different market
   - Less established competition

4. **Policy Support**
   - Government allowing large employers to become providers
   - Trend is growing
   - Early mover advantage

### Critical Questions

1. **Market Validation:**
   - Have you spoken to any large employer providers?
   - What are their biggest challenges?
   - Would they pay for this?
   - How much?

2. **Competition:**
   - Does AELP serve this market?
   - Are there other networks?
   - What do they currently do for support?

3. **Value Proposition:**
   - What specific help do they need?
   - Is it compliance? Quality? Operations?
   - Can you deliver it?

4. **Pricing:**
   - What's the right price point?
   - Corporate budgets = higher, but what's the limit?
   - What's the value justification?

3. **Large Market**

   - Corporate budgets
   - Growing market (policy-driven)

4. **Unique Position**
   - Preemptive = different from reactive support
   - Help them decide IF they should become providers

### Financial Model Structure

**Revenue Model Options:**

**Option A: Annual Membership**
- Price: £X per year
- Includes: 4 events, access to network, resources
- Pros: Predictable revenue, easier to sell
- Cons: Need to deliver value all year

**Option B: Per Event**
- Price: £X per event
- Includes: One event, access to network
- Pros: Lower commitment, easier to start
- Cons: Unpredictable revenue, harder to scale

**Option C: Tiered Membership**
- Basic: £X (events only)
- Premium: £Y (events + resources + consulting)
- Pros: Multiple price points, upsell opportunity
- Cons: More complex, harder to explain
