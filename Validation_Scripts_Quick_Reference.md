## 💬 CONVERSATION FLOW (Once They Agree)

### Opening (2 minutes):
- Thank them for their time
- Brief context: "17 years in training, exploring new opportunity"
- Set expectation: "Not selling, just understanding need"
- Ask permission to ask questions

### Awareness Check (2 minutes):
1. "Are you aware of the Growth and Skills Levy and apprenticeship units launching April 2026?"
   - If no: Brief explanation
   - If yes: "What's your understanding?"

### Current Situation (3 minutes):
2. "How are you currently using your apprenticeship levy?"
3. "What are your biggest challenges with the current system?"

### Interest Check (3 minutes):
4. "Are you considering using apprenticeship units when they launch?"
5. "Would you consider becoming a provider to use your levy directly?"
6. "What are your concerns about this?"

### Need Assessment (4 minutes):
7. "What support would you need to make this decision?"
8. "What information do you need?"
9. "What are your biggest questions?"

### Value Test (3 minutes):
10. "Would you value a network of other large employers going through similar decisions?"
11. "What would make such a network valuable to you?"
12. "What topics would you want covered?"

### Pricing Test (2 minutes):
13. "What would you be willing to pay for this kind of support?"
14. "Would you pay £X per year for a network like this?"
15. "What's the value to you?"

### Commitment Check (1 minute):
16. "Would you attend a free event to learn more?"
17. "Would you be interested in joining if we launch?"

### Closing:
- Thank them for their time
- Offer to share findings/research
- Ask if they know others who might be interested
- Set follow-up if appropriate

## 🎯 KEY PRINCIPLES

### Do:
✅ Be transparent about who you are
✅ Leverage your 17 years of experience
✅ Position as research/exploration
✅ Provide value (insights, findings)
✅ Ask for help, not sales
✅ Respect their time
✅ Follow up with findings

### Don't:
❌ Use fake identity/brand
❌ Lie about purpose
❌ Pressure or sell

## 🎯 SUCCESS METRICS

### Validation is Successful If:
- ✅ 30%+ response rate to outreach
- ✅ 50%+ show interest in network
- ✅ Clear pricing feedback (range)
- ✅ Identified pain points
- ✅ 5+ people commit to advisory board
- ✅ 20+ people interested in free event
- ✅ Clear value proposition emerges

### Red Flags:
- ❌ <10% response rate (wrong approach or market)
- ❌ No interest in network
- ❌ Pricing objections (too expensive)
- ❌ "We already have this" (competition)
- ❌ "Not a priority" (timing issue)

## 💡 QUICK TIPS

1. **Start with People You Know:**
   - Easier to get response
   - More honest feedback
   - Builds momentum

2. **Be Brief:**
   - 15 minutes, not 60
   - Respect their time
   - Get to the point

3. **Provide Value:**
   - Share insights
   - Offer findings
   - Help them understand policy

4. **Follow Up:**
   - Thank them
   - Share findings
   - Keep them informed
   - Build relationship

5. **Track Everything:**
   - Notes from calls
   - Common themes
   - Pricing feedback
   - Objections
