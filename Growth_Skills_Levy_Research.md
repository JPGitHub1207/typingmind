## Current Apprenticeship Levy System (Baseline Understanding)

### What is the Apprenticeship Levy?
- Introduced in April 2017
- 0.5% payroll tax on employers with annual pay bill over £3 million
- Paid into digital account
- Can be used to fund apprenticeship training and assessment
- Funds expire after 24 months if not used

### Traditional Levy Usage
- **Full Apprenticeships Only:** Levy funds typically used for complete apprenticeship standards
- **Provider-Based:** Employers usually transfer funds to training providers
- **Restrictions:** Limited flexibility in how funds can be used

### Key Limitations (Traditional System)
- Funds tied to full apprenticeship standards
- Must use approved training providers
- Limited ability to use for modular/short courses
- Funds expire if not used within 24 months

## Requirements for Employers to Become Providers

### Current Understanding (Needs Verification)

To become a registered training provider, employers typically need:

1. **Ofsted Registration:**
   - Register with Ofsted as training provider
   - Meet quality standards
   - Pass inspection (can be Grade 4 = failure, as you mentioned Citron example)

2. **DfE Registration:**
   - Register with Department for Education
   - Meet funding rules
   - Comply with apprenticeship funding requirements

3. **Awarding Body/EPA Registration:**
   - Register with awarding bodies
   - Meet end-point assessment requirements
   - Comply with standards

4. **Quality Management:**
   - Quality Manager role (as you mentioned)
   - Compliance systems
   - Audit readiness

5. **Funding/Compliance:**
   - Funding Manager role (as you mentioned)
   - DfE compliance
   - Audit processes
