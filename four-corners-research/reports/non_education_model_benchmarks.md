# Non-education model benchmarks

Recorded 5 October 2026. Every organisation in this file sells outside education, or at least not as an education specialist.

These prices are evidence of how a retainer, a points system or an outsourced department can be built. They are not evidence of what a college, a training provider or a school will pay.

## Gripped

B2B SaaS demand generation, London. The pricing page, fetched 5 October 2026, says from £3,500 a month plus project fees for guidance, from £7,500 a month for one or two channels, and from £15,000 a month for full demand generation. VAT status is not published. The FAQ on the same day says retainers typically start around £4,000 to £6,000 a month for single-channel work and go to £15,000 to £20,000+ for a full programme. Both texts are official. They are not averaged here.

What is interesting is the boundary of the fee. Paid media is extra. The FAQ recommends at least £5,000 a month on LinkedIn and £3,000 to £5,000 on Google before paid media is worth starting. They do not take a percentage of spend. Website rebuilds are extra, from £18,000 to £45,000 for a refresh and £45,000 to £150,000+ for a redesign. An onboarding fee is typical. Its amount is not published. The FAQ asks for about three months, then three months’ notice, and refuses a 30-day pilot. Work runs in 30-day sprints against 90-day objectives. One account lead sits in front of in-house specialists. Weekly or fortnightly meetings, plus a quarterly review.

**What Four Corners could adapt.** A written split between the monthly fee, media spend and a one-off build. A refusal to sell a pilot that is too short to learn from. One named lead, with specialists behind that person.

**What not to infer.** An education buyer will not be shown to pay £7,500 or £15,000 a month by this page. Gripped’s own fit is Series A and B SaaS, often £2 million to £20 million ARR, with existing ad budget. Four Corners’ £8,000 managed test sits below Gripped’s published full-service floor. That is a packaging fact about Gripped, not a ceiling or a target for training providers.

## Greenlight Web

Manchester agency for B2B, professional services, engineering and manufacturing. This is not Greenlight Digital at greenlightdigital.uk, which published no retainer on the page reviewed, and it is not GREENLIGHT UK LIMITED 03567949.

The full-service page publishes three prices and three hour caps. £2,000 a month buys up to 25 hours. £3,500 a month buys up to 45 hours. £5,000 a month buys up to 75 hours. VAT status is not published. Every service is available on every tier. The buyer points the hours. A dedicated account manager plans the month. New website builds are quoted separately. Minimum terms are agreed upfront. The length is not published.

**What Four Corners could adapt.** Selling capacity rather than a frozen channel menu. Putting the hours on the page so a £2,000 fee has a meaning. Keeping a website build out of the monthly cap.

**What not to infer.** These are not education prices. The hours are a cap, not a promise that every hour is a senior strategist. Dividing £2,000 by 25 gives £80. That arithmetic is not a rate Greenlight Web publishes. A search snippet mentioned a £1,000 tier. It was not on the page that was fetched, so it is not recorded.

## Spectra Media

Chester. Pricing page fetched 5 October 2026. Establish is from £3,000 a month. Grow is from £6,000 a month. Scale is from £12,000, and that heading does not repeat “per month”. Every tier includes strategy, content, social, SEO, design and ads. The higher tiers add pace. Custom automation starts at Grow. A named strategic lead and board-ready reporting sit on Scale. Ad spend is paid by the client to the platform. Print is at cost. Hours are not published.

Guarantee-backed plans run for 12 months. There is a month-six checkpoint with no penalty if agreed leading indicators are missed. If agreed enquiry targets are missed, Spectra keeps working at its own cost. The guarantee does not cover one-off projects. A separate page illustrates £3,000 a month as £36,000 over twelve months. A free 30-minute call produces a scoped quote.

**What Four Corners could adapt.** One menu, three speeds. A checkpoint inside a longer term, instead of a lock-in with no door. A guarantee that is specific about the metric and about what is excluded. Automation treated as the thing that justifies the step up, not another vague “more support”.

**What not to infer.** A training provider has not been shown to accept a 12-month marketing guarantee or a from-£3,000 floor. Spectra does not publish the hours inside £3,000, so it cannot be lined up against a 24-hour advisory month except as a fee comparison, which is the wrong comparison.

## Digital Litmus

A 2025 pricing PDF, fetched 5 October 2026. B2B growth, HubSpot and RevOps. Example packages are Starter 64 points at £5,800, Grow 80 points at £7,200 with an unexplained asterisk, Accelerate 102 points at £9,200, and MAX 163 points at £14,700. The PDF does not print “per month” beside those four figures. Continuous improvement, under websites, does: £1,500, £2,375, £3,150 and £4,250 per month for 15 to 50 points. HubSpot onboarding starts at £2,000. Hub set-up starts at £3,000. RevOps starts at £1,500 a month. Websites are £8,500 to £23,500 by page count. VAT status is not published.

Work runs in 90-day sprints. Points can move up or down after a sprint. A dedicated account manager and a quarterly review are on the examples. A free assessment is the entry. The assessment’s output is not described.

**What Four Corners could adapt.** A unit the client can reallocate, with a sprint review as the moment the volume changes. A project price for implementation that is not disguised as the first month of a retainer. RevOps as a separate monthly floor from campaign production.

**What not to infer.** Do not turn £5,800 into a monthly education fee. The PDF does not say that. Do not treat a HubSpot onboarding floor of £2,000 as comparable to HubGem’s education onboarding, which starts from £10,560 + VAT. They are different scopes until someone publishes the hours.

## The distinction, once

Skills Marketing’s from-£3,500, £6,000 and £8,500 a month are education-buyer prices, in the direct-competitor file. Greenlight Web, Spectra, Gripped and Digital Litmus are not. A similarity of packaging is not a similarity of willingness to pay.
