## What they actually sell

**Bids and register entry.** ESS Assist sells a shop. On 5 October 2026 the consultancy page listed a one-hour session at £120 and a two-hour session at £220, under a header of From £120.00 + VAT. The shop archive the same day described Stuart Atkinson’s session as discounted to £99.00 + VAT. Both figures are live descriptions. They are not one price. Bid review is £595.00 and RoATP bid review is £795.00 on the session page. Those two lines do not repeat the plus-VAT wording. The guide is £49.99 + VAT. LVP account support is £500.00 + VAT and LVP registration support is £995.00 + VAT, each for contract values up to £50k. The GLA London Talent Pathway line is an initial deposit of £1,995.00 + VAT. The balance is not on that line. Tender Tracker, on a page not re-opened on 5 October 2026, was £39.99 a month or £399 a year, plus VAT, minimum 12 months, with a four-week trial.

The Education and Skills Consultancy, re-fetched the same day, publishes main provider £4,500 + VAT, supporting provider £3,600 + VAT, and employer provider £4,500 + VAT. The fee includes a bid writer, narrative responses, policy review, templates for missing policies, entry on the platform and a quality check. A free consultation is the door. A 100% pass-rate sentence is a marketing claim.

SCLO Consulting offers the same kind of bid support. The FAQ reviewed on 2 October 2026 published no fee. That page was not re-opened on 5 October 2026.

**Provider commercial numeracy, not campaigns.** SDN’s financial series is three webinars of two hours, £245.00 + VAT, or £350.00 + VAT with a one-hour consultancy call. The buyer receives recordings, slides, an attendance certificate, a costing tool, a forecast tool and an RPL calculator. A group discount is offered by email and not priced. This trains the provider to price apprenticeships. It does not find the employer.

**School marketers’ association training.** AMCIS workshops were last published at £185 for members and £285 for non-members, VAT status not published, on a page whose dates had already passed by 2 October 2026. The marketing diploma’s price was not on the page. The Independent Schools Association Marketing Works day on 13 October 2026 is £140 for members and £170 for non-members, VAT status not published. These are training days for in-house school staff. They are not agency retainers, and they are not ITP prices.

## What Four Corners can use

**Analysis.**

The bid market already has a ladder: a £49.99 guide, an hour around £99 to £220 + VAT, a review around £595 to £795, a deposit that can be £1,995 + VAT, and a finished APAR application at £3,600 or £4,500 + VAT. Four Corners can learn the shape. It should not copy the fees into a marketing partnership.

SDN prints the training price and the training-plus-one-hour price on the same page. The step from £245 to £350 + VAT is the whole consultancy upsell. That is a cleaner commercial move than hiding the clinic.

Association days at £140 to £285 show what a school marketer pays a membership body. They do not show what that school pays an agency, and they do not show what a training provider pays for employer acquisition.
