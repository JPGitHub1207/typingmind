# Pricing and packaging lessons

Recorded 5 October 2026. Education prices and non-education model prices are kept apart. A “from” price is a floor. VAT wording is only what the page printed. Hours are recorded only when the page stated them.

**Analysis** paragraphs are judgement about design. They are not a recommendation to copy a fee.

## Architectures that are actually published

**Education buyers, direct competitors**

Skills Marketing publishes monthly floors for training providers, apprenticeship providers and FE colleges: from £3,500, from £6,000 and from £8,500 a month, plus a social ladder from £1,800 and from £2,200, £3,800 and £6,000 a month. VAT status is not published. Hours, minimum term and media-spend rules are not published. The audit is described as fixed-price and the amount is not published.

McCracken publishes general UK figures that are also offered to training providers: advisory from £1,500 a month, an embedded description of £3,000 to £6,000 a month, a review from £1,500, a strategy day from £1,250, and interim cover from £500 a day. VAT status is not published. The training-provider page does not have a different fee. His FAQ’s one to two days a week and the training page’s two to four days a month are not reconciled.

HubGem publishes education HubSpot work plus VAT: masterclass £1,175, onboarding from £10,560, £12,533, £14,126 and £14,099, re-onboarding from £4,420, LIVE early bird £190. The masterclass is the only product in the direct set with a published hour stack: 6 hours of calls, 2 hours of implementation and 1 hour of project management.

School prices, which are a different buyer: Attenger £350 per person for 4 hours, £645, £995, £1,295, playbook £697, SLT pack £2,497. VAT status not published on those. Stickman £1,996 + VAT for two people on a January-sale page still live on 5 October 2026, plus £1,125 + VAT for an extra person. Chameleon Winning Bids £950 + VAT or £1,250 + VAT. Sticky Note from £100 for campaign set-up and from £500 a day, VAT status not published. The Creative Partner retainer is not priced.

Projects Marketing, Beanstalk, Blueberry, FE Business, MarCommEd, Creative Collins, Marketing 4 Success, Tillison and AmbleGlow sell the service and do not publish the fee.

**Adjacent, not marketing retainers**

ESS Assist sessions £120 and £220 + VAT on the product page, and a shop line discounted to £99 + VAT. Reviews £595 and £795, with VAT wording not repeated on those lines. APAR writing £3,600 + VAT or £4,500 + VAT. SDN £245 + VAT for six live hours, or £350 + VAT with one more hour.

**Model benchmarks, not education prices**

Greenlight Web: £2,000 for up to 25 hours, £3,500 for up to 45, £5,000 for up to 75. VAT status not published.

Spectra Media: from £3,000 a month, from £6,000 a month, from £12,000 on the top tier. Twelve-month term. Hours not published.

## Hours, retainers, credits

Where hours are published, they belong to a workshop or a webinar, or to Greenlight Web’s cap. No education retainer in this study publishes both the monthly fee and the hours inside it.

McCracken publishes days, not hours, and the two day-counts disagree across pages.

Digital Litmus publishes points. The client can move points between jobs, and the firm can change the number of points after a 90-day sprint. That is the only worked credits model in the set. The four headline package prices are not labelled monthly, so they are not treated as retainers.

**Analysis.** A retainer without hours forces the buyer to trust the word “department”. A hour cap without a seniority mix forces the buyer to trust that a 25-hour month contains judgement, not only production. Four Corners’ test offers currently state hours. Almost nobody in the education sample does. That is a difference. It is also a promise that has to be deliverable, because the comparable firms avoided making it.

## Good, better, best

Three different mechanisms show up.

Skills Marketing changes the work. Foundation is one channel and a fortnightly contact. Growth is multi-channel and weekly. Full Department adds brand, PR, events and board contact. The social products are a second ladder, not a fourth tier of the same ladder.

Spectra and Greenlight Web keep the menu and change the pace. Spectra adds automation at £6,000 and a named lead at the top. Greenlight Web changes only the hour cap. Gripped changes the scope: guidance, then one or two channels, then the whole engine.

Chameleon’s Winning Bids price changes with the size of the room: £950 + VAT up to five people, £1,250 + VAT for a larger regional or MAT group. The online version stays at £950 + VAT.

**Analysis.** A buyer can understand “more hours” and “more channels” more easily than “more partnership”. The education firm that publishes numbers, Skills Marketing, uses channels and contact frequency. The firms that publish hours use the cap.

## What creates a higher price

Published, not inferred:

- More channels and closer contact, on Skills Marketing’s own labels.
- More days, on McCracken’s two descriptions, which do not match each other.
- More of the portal build, on HubGem, until the enterprise from-price, which is slightly below Elevate and is a different scope.
- More seats, on Stickman, after two people are already in the fee.
- A larger private group, on Chameleon’s bid day.
- Applicant type, on APAR fees. A supporting provider pays less than a main provider.
- An extra consultancy hour, on SDN, from £245 to £350 + VAT.
- Pace and automation, on Spectra.
- Hour cap, on Greenlight Web.
- Scope of channels, on Gripped.
- Number of points, on Digital Litmus.

Things that were named as extra rather than inside the fee: Gripped and Spectra ad spend; Sticky Note campaign budget; Gripped and Greenlight Web website builds; Digital Litmus HubSpot set-up as its own project; ESS writing balances above a deposit.

## Minimum terms

Published terms are scarce in education. Skills Marketing, McCracken, HubGem, Attenger and the telemarketing firms do not publish a minimum term on the pages used. ESS Tender Tracker, on the older page, is 12 months. Gripped’s FAQ says usually three months, then three months’ notice, and no mid-contract pause. Spectra’s guarantee plans are 12 months, with a month-six exit if agreed leading indicators are missed.

**Analysis.** The long term appears where the firm is selling a guarantee or a data subscription. It is not the default on the education pages that publish a fee.

## What £2,000, £4,000 and £8,000 buy in the pages that mention nearby numbers

These are nearby published numbers. They are not Four Corners prices, and the non-education rows are not education evidence.

**Near £2,000**

- Greenlight Web, not education: £2,000 a month, up to 25 hours, every service, account manager, website builds extra. VAT status not published.
- McCracken, general rate also offered to training providers: strategic advisory from £1,500 a month. Channel work stays with the client. Days per month are not stated for that floor.
- Stickman, schools: £1,996 + VAT for a six-module course for two people, on the January-sale page. Not a month of delivery.
- Attenger, schools: SLT Strategy Pack £2,497, VAT status not published, for board materials and a 45-minute call.
- Digital Litmus, not education: RevOps from £1,500 a month, and continuous improvement at £1,500 a month for 15 points. HubSpot onboarding starts at £2,000 as a project.
- SDN, education but not marketing: £245 + VAT buys six hours of teaching and tools.

**Near £4,000**

- Skills Marketing, same buyer as Four Corners: Foundation from £3,500 a month. One lead-generation channel, fortnightly contact. Hours not published.
- Spectra, not education: Establish from £3,000 a month for the full menu at the slow pace, 12-month term, no custom automation.
- Greenlight Web, not education: £3,500 a month, up to 45 hours.
- Gripped, not education: the pricing-page floor is from £3,500 a month for guidance and separate projects. The FAQ floor is about £4,000 to £6,000. Full channel execution on the pricing page starts at £7,500.
- Education and Skills Consultancy: £3,600 + VAT or £4,500 + VAT buys a finished APAR application, not a marketing month.
- Skills Marketing’s Channel Management, a social-media tier, is from £3,800 a month for posting and light community management.

**Near £8,000**

- Skills Marketing Full Department is from £8,500 a month. Growth, the middle department offer, is from £6,000. Hours are not published. VAT status is not published.
- Spectra Grow is from £6,000 a month and adds automation. Scale starts from £12,000 and adds a named lead. There is no £8,000 tier.
- Greenlight Web stops at £5,000 for up to 75 hours. A blog sentence about £8,000 describes other agencies’ rigid packages. It is not Greenlight Web’s price.
- Gripped’s full engine starts from £15,000 a month on the pricing page.
- HubGem onboarding starts from £10,560 + VAT. That is a project, not a month.

**Analysis.** In the education sample, the only firm that publishes a ladder through this band is Skills Marketing, and it publishes floors without hours. In the non-education sample, £2,000 buys a small hour cap, £4,000-ish buys either a larger cap or the start of a full menu, and £8,000 is not itself a published tier. The next published step is often £12,000 or £15,000, still not an education price.
