# UK education training market summary

Verified 2 October 2026. Every price and hour below is taken from `education_training_products.csv`. Cells marked `NOT PUBLISHED` are left out of the ranges. VAT wording is copied as published and is not converted. Where a figure is published without VAT wording, the range says so.

The database holds 38 organisations and 103 products. Three organisations were added because an official page showed a current education offer: The Stickman Consultancy, Chameleon Consultancy & Training, and the Independent Schools Association. LLSE, UCL Institute of Education and Church of England NPQ provision are still unverified. Teacher Development Trust has no current standalone product: its homepage says it is now part of the Chartered College of Teaching.

This is a training-supply map for education buyers. It is not the buyer-side L&D market in `Four_Corners_Strategic_Research_Report.md`.

## 9. Where training is commoditised

The commodity pattern is a certificate, a short module and a low per-person price, with the long list of near-identical courses sitting inside one commercial model.

- High Speed Training education safeguarding titles are £20 + VAT to £65 + VAT, with bulk discounts from 10 percent at 10 courses to 40 percent at 500. https://www.highspeedtraining.co.uk/campaign/kcsie/
- NSPCC school safeguarding is £35, and the refresher falls to £20 at 100 or more places.
- EduCare, The National College and The Key sell the library as an annual licence or quote. National College and The Key do not publish a list price.
- nasen Plus at £19.99 and Academy from £375 + VAT are the same shape in SEND.
- Chartered College on-demand CPD is included in a monthly fee of a few pounds.

These products do not include a review of the buyer's own pipeline, bid or employer offer.

## 10. Where buyers pay a premium

Premium here means a published price well above the commodity certificate, attached to the buyer's own situation, a scarce cohort, or done-for-you work.

- Hartz education days, from £1,500 + VAT and £2,500 + VAT, against Attenger's £350 open session.
- HubGem onboarding, from £10,560 + VAT, against the £1,175 + VAT masterclass.
- APAR done-for-you, £3,600 + VAT to £4,500 + VAT, against ESS Assist's £995 + VAT review deposit.
- ASCL's nine-month trust CEO programme, £3,115 + VAT for members, limited places.
- Teach First's executive NPQ, £3,890 plus VAT, for 18 months.
- Stickman's £1,996 + VAT course for two people, against ISA's £140 to £170 open day. The Stickman figure is on a page titled January sale and has no crossed-out list price.
- SSAT secondary membership at £1,795 + VAT, against primary at £485 + VAT, because the bundle includes programme places.

## 11. Most scalable models in this file

Scalable means the published design can add buyers without adding a matching day of expert time.

- Per-learner catalogues and annual licences: High Speed Training, NSPCC, EduCare, nasen Academy, National College, The Key.
- Individual professional membership: Chartered College, ISBL, SET, NGA.
- Self-paced playbooks: Attenger at £697.
- Member-included modules: Jisc at £50 + VAT only for non-members.
- Licensed delivery: Autism Education Trust publishes no national delegate fee. Partners deliver and may charge. That scales the IP, and it also hides the price.

NPQs scale through delivery partners, but the self-fund prices and the published clinic patterns show a high facilitation load. They are not a lightweight product.

## 12. Most defensible models for a small specialist

Defensible means a buyer can see a tangible output tied to their own organisation, and a large catalogue cannot copy it by adding another module.

- A short taught session plus the buyer's own document, as in ASCL's pupil-premium strategy review inside a £425 + VAT place, or SDN's £350 + VAT series-plus-call.
- A playbook with tools, as in Attenger's £697 playbook, which is explicitly not done-for-you.
- A done-for-you application with templates, as in APAR support at £3,600 + VAT to £4,500 + VAT. The defence is the outcome and the provider's record, not the workshop.
- A cohort with peer visits and a toolkit, as in ASCL's executive programme. It does not scale, which is part of the defence.
- ISBL's OpEx ladder, where each level is a prerequisite for the next and the price includes accreditation.

A small specialist cannot defend a £31 certificate. The file shows that price already exists, with bulk discounts.

## 13. Gaps in the ITP and FE commercial-growth market

The ITP and FE rows are thick on funding, compliance, Ofsted preparation and register applications. They are thin on priced help with employer pipeline, messaging and account growth.

- FE Business names sales, key-account and employer-engagement programmes. Price, duration and outputs are not published. https://febusiness.co.uk/
- SCLO and ESS Assist price, or at least productise, bids and APAR. They do not publish an employer-campaign or CRM product.
- SDN prices a finance series and tools. The wider compliance packages have no price in this file.
- LX Digital states that subscription rates exist and the numbers did not render. Apprenticeship Mastery has no price.
- No ITP row publishes a competitor-marketing review, a website and enquiry review, or an employer account-growth review with a fee and an output.
- Open workshop prices that do exist in this market are the SDN £245 + VAT series and the ETF £445 leadership course, not a commercial-growth workshop.

School marketing is more priced than ITP commercial growth. Independent-school courses publish £140 to £1,295. The apprenticeship-provider equivalents are mostly "contact us".
