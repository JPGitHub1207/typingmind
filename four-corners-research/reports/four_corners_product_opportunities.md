# Four Corners product opportunities

This report compares Four Corners' named working offers with the education training products in the CSVs. Competitor prices and hours are only those published in `education_training_products.csv`. Nothing here is a recommendation to copy another provider's delivery. Lines marked **HYPOTHESIS** are judgements about a test price, not facts from a competitor page.

Four Corners' own £295, £395, £595, £2,000, £4,000 and £8,000 figures are the working offer names. They are not competitor evidence.

## How the market sits against those offers

Short open courses with a published fee cluster from about £140 to £445. Examples: Independent Schools Association Marketing Works at £140 member and £170 non-member, VAT unstated, on 13 October 2026; Attenger's four-hour AI session at £350 per person, VAT unstated; ASCL's disadvantaged-provision programme at £425 + VAT for members; ETF Introducing Leadership at £445, VAT unstated, for 9 hours.

Practical products with tools or implementation sit higher. Attenger's playbook is £697, VAT unstated. HubGem's AI masterclass is £1,175 + VAT for six calls plus implementation hours. Stickman's marketing or admissions course is £1,996 + VAT for two people, on a page titled January sale. SDN's finance series is £245 + VAT for six hours and three tools, or £350 + VAT with a one-hour call.

Done-for-you and long cohorts sit from the low thousands upward. APAR support is £3,600 + VAT or £4,500 + VAT. ASCL's trust CEO programme is £3,115 + VAT for members across September 2026 to May 2027. Teach First's executive NPQ is £3,890 plus VAT for 18 months. HubGem re-onboarding starts at £4,420 + VAT, and full onboarding starts at £10,560 + VAT.

Commodity certificates sit at £20 + VAT to £65 + VAT (High Speed Training) or £25 to £35 with VAT unstated (NSPCC). They are not the comparison set for a commercial-growth offer.

The ITP and FE commercial-growth gap is the useful one. FE Business, SCLO Consulting, Creative Collins and MarCommEd describe employer engagement, bids or marketing workshops and do not publish a fee. Priced neighbours are ESS Assist, SDN, Chameleon's bid masterclass and The Education and Skills Consultancy's APAR service.

## Free Contributor webinar

Closest published patterns are Stickman's free two-minute health check, nasen's free Core membership, and Jisc's member-included AI module at £50 + VAT for non-members. HubGem LIVE has an early-bird price of £190 + VAT, so a live event in this market is not automatically free. LX Group's accelerator is called free only on a founder's LinkedIn post.

No education provider in the file publishes a contributor webinar that names the next paid product and its price on the same page. Attenger's £350 session is the clearest cheap paid step after free prompts and a newsletter.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A free webinar is a lead, not a product, if the thank-you page offers one priced next step under £500. The file supports a next step in the £140 to £350 open-course band, not a jump to a partnership.

Test: one webinar, one URL, one paid next step, and a count of how many attendees book it within 14 days.

Risk: several "free" routes in the file are scholarships or memberships, not a commercial sample. A webinar with no priced next step cannot be compared with this evidence.

## Commercial Growth Review, Scorecard, Diagnostic and Four Corner Brief

No row publishes a scored commercial-growth report as its own SKU.

Nearest tangible outputs:

- Attenger's £350 session teaches a visibility audit and enquiry reporting, and the buyer leaves with templates and an action plan. It is not a written audit.
- Attenger's £697 playbook includes dashboard templates and an ROI calculator. The buyer builds the outputs.
- SDN's £245 + VAT series supplies three tools. The £350 + VAT version adds one hour on the provider's own numbers.
- ASCL reviews a pupil premium strategy inside a £425 + VAT member place. That is a document review, not a marketing diagnostic.
- EduCare and nasen Academy include reporting suites or dashboards inside an annual licence, from £410 and from £375 + VAT.

ESS Assist sells an hour at £120 + VAT or two hours at £220 + VAT. That is the closest published price for a short expert session, and it is not a report.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A one-off diagnostic with a written scorecard has no direct published competitor price. A test price between the £220 two-hour session and the £350 to £445 short courses is the narrowest band the file supports. A diagnostic above £700 starts to overlap Attenger's playbook and needs a finished report the playbook does not provide.

Test: sell the scorecard once, without a workshop, and record whether buyers pay for a document or only for a meeting.

Risk: if the scorecard is a questionnaire with no provider-specific finding, it will be compared with free health checks and free nasen membership.

## Practical guides and checklists

Attenger's playbook at £697 is the priced guide. It includes prompts, sequences, a calendar, dashboards and an ROI calculator, plus email support aimed at two working days and three months of update emails. It says it is not done-for-you.

ESS Assist sells a guide, "How to become a publicly funded training provider", at £49.99 + VAT. That is the commodity end of a guide.

Chameleon's Marketing Essentials at £150 + VAT includes templates and about four hours of content for 12 months.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A checklist given away free matches the lead-gen pattern. A guide sold alone is evidenced at £49.99 + VAT for a narrow how-to, or £697 where it is an operating system. A Four Corners guide priced near £700 needs the same specificity as the playbook. A short checklist should not carry that price.

Test: offer the checklist free on the webinar, and the longer guide only after someone has attended or bought a workshop.

Risk: the £697 figure is VAT-unstated and is one provider. It is not a market rate.

## £295 workshop

Published open-course neighbours, different VAT status, so they are not one range:

- ISA, one day, £140 member and £170 non-member, VAT unstated.
- Chameleon bid-writing half day, £250, VAT unstated, stated on a blog.
- AMCIS one-day workshops, £185 member and £285 non-member, VAT unstated, dates on that page had passed.
- Attenger, 4 hours, £350, VAT unstated.
- ASCL short programme, £425 + VAT member.

Hours at the £350 point are 4. Hours at the £445 ETF course are 9. ISA's day is 9.30am to 3pm including breaks.

**HYPOTHESIS.** £295 sits between the ISA day and Attenger's four-hour session. It is plausible for a half day or a short live workshop with templates, and it is high for a certificate course. It is low if the session includes a review of the buyer's own materials.

Test: one public date, a named template pack, and a cap on places. Compare conversion with the free webinar.

Risk: AMCIS prices may be a lapsed tariff. Do not treat £185 to £285 as a live booking price.

## £395 workshop plus implementation clinic

The file's clearest "course plus a short applied extra" is SDN: £245 + VAT for the series, £350 + VAT once a one-hour call is added. The extra hour is £105 + VAT on that page.

Attenger's four-hour session is £350 without a later clinic. ASCL's £425 + VAT member place already includes a strategy review, spread over two twilights and a day.

**HYPOTHESIS.** £395 for a workshop plus one clinic is consistent with SDN's step from £245 to £350 and with Attenger's £350 session if the clinic is short. It is only a different product from the £295 workshop if the clinic uses the buyer's own material.

Test: sell the £295 workshop and the £395 version on the same page, and see whether the clinic is what people pay the extra for.

Risk: clinic length is not fixed by competitor evidence. SDN's extra is one published hour. A vague "clinic" will be compared with that hour.

## £595 workshop, clinic and review

No row is a three-part stack at £595.

Neighbours: Attenger social programmes at £995 and £1,295 per person for four weeks, VAT unstated; HubGem masterclass at £1,175 + VAT with implementation hours; ASCL's review-inclusive programme at £425 + VAT; Attenger playbook at £697.

**HYPOTHESIS.** £595 is below the multi-week social programmes and below the playbook, and above a single review-inclusive short programme. It can be tested only if the review is a written note on the buyer's campaign, website or pipeline, not a second conversation.

Test: define the review as a fixed page count or a fixed set of findings before selling it. If buyers will not pay £595, the £395 stack is the one the file supports more closely.

Risk: without a visible artefact, £595 will be judged against a £350 to £445 training day.

Risk: Stickman's £1,996 price may be a sale price. The list price is not on the main course page.

ISA's £140 to £170 day is about marketing ROI and case studies, not a review of the delegate's own channels.

## Employer Proposition and Messaging, and Employer Campaign Pack

Chameleon's Winning Bids masterclass is £950 + VAT for an individual school or small group, and £1,250 + VAT for a larger MAT or regional group, including an online version at £950 + VAT. That is messaging for bids, not for employers.

FE Business's employer-engagement programmes and Creative Collins's strategy workshops describe this job and have no price.

Attenger's playbook includes copy and email sequences at £697, self-serve.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A campaign pack of messages and assets, without done-for-you distribution, sits nearer the £697 playbook than the £950 bid masterclass. A pack that includes the first send, the landing page or the employer list is closer to consultancy and should not use the playbook price.

Test: sell the pack as files the provider sends themselves. Record whether they then ask for the sending to be done for them.

Risk: bid-writing buyers and employer-marketing buyers are not shown to be the same budget.

## Practical Playbook

The direct comparison is Attenger's AI Playbook at £697 one-time, VAT unstated: tools, a 90-day plan, email support, and an explicit limit that it is not done-for-you. The SLT pack adds a 45-minute call. Its price is not published in the product row.

ESS Assist's £49.99 + VAT guide is the cheap alternative for a single topic.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A playbook without human support can be tested near £697 only if the contents are as specific as prompts, calculators and a 90-day plan. A thinner checklist should follow the £49.99 evidence instead.

Test: publish the contents list and the support limit in the same place as the price.

Risk: one VAT-unstated price is not a band.

Test: do not publish £8,000 until one buyer has paid £4,000 and asked for work that was out of scope.

Risk: inventing a price in the gap between £4,500 and £10,560 is the main unsupported step in the ladder.

These are the client-product campaigns. The file shows how providers buy help to win this work, not a price for running the campaign itself.

- Chameleon's bid masterclass at £950 + VAT to £1,250 + VAT is the priced training version of "win a bid", for schools rather than ITPs.

**HYPOTHESIS.** A launch campaign that includes the application is in the £1,495 to £4,500 evidence, and those products are bid production, not ongoing employer marketing. An employer campaign after the provider is already on the register has no published price in this file. Price it only after the review products above have a test result.

Test: one apprenticeship provider, one campaign type, a fixed asset list, and a separate line for any bid writing.

Risk: treating a Skills Bootcamp campaign as if it had the same evidence as an APAR application would overstate what the file shows.

## What to test first

The file is strongest around short practical sessions, a self-serve playbook, and a single applied hour. It is weakest around retained partnerships and ITP employer campaigns.

A sensible first public test, as a hypothesis rather than a finding, is the free webinar with one next step in the £295 to £395 band, plus a written review only if the buyer brings their own material. Hold the £4,000 and £8,000 partnerships until a buyer has paid for a defined sprint or a defined pack.

Missing evidence that would change these tests: FE Business prices, Creative Collins day rates, SCLO project fees, a current Stickman list price, HubGem's dated retainer page, and any ITP that publishes an employer-campaign fee.
