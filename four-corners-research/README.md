# Four Corners strategic research

Independent UK market sizing, analogue competitor analysis, and commercial challenge of the Four Corners working concept.

**Read this:** [Four_Corners_Strategic_Research_Report.md](./Four_Corners_Strategic_Research_Report.md)

The report is written to a consulting standard. It does not validate the concept. Headline findings:

- **11,415** UK enterprises have 250+ employees (ONS/Nomis, March 2025).
- Realistic year-3 SOM is **£0.8–1.8 million**, not a category-defining marketplace.
- **Do not launch** free managed learning, public programme reviews, or a resource library.
- Recommended beachhead: paid peer councils plus decision-record shortlisting for UK Heads of L&D in organisations with **1,000–9,999** employees.
