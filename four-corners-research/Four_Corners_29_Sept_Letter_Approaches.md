# 29 September — three letter approaches (robot handwriting)

Agency robot, signed by James. Same merge, same facts, three tones. Pick **one** body for Wave A. Recipients will compare; do not mix approaches in the same post.

**Do not** say you wrote these by hand. **Do not** mention membership, club, or 25 August.

## Production (all three)

**Merge fields only:** `[Name]` and `[Company]`. No job-history personalisation. Thin extra fields look fake when two Heads of L&D compare.

**Handwritten body:** the version you pick. Short on purpose. Robot script reads worse past ~140 words.

**Printed footer (not handwriting):** QR + short URL + reply line. Mailrooms photocopy; some people will not scan. Put both.

Suggested footer:

> Tuesday 29 September 2026 · 14:00–17:30 · Chiswell Street Dining Rooms, EC1  
> Scan for the afternoon · fourcornersclub.co.uk  
> £400 + VAT · reply to this letter also works · jamespearson1207@gmail.com

Point the QR at the **paid 29 September page**, not the old club/home template. Do not post until that page is live.

**Envelope:** their name, title, company. Same hand as the letter if the agency offers it. Four Corners does not need to be on the front.

## Which to send

| | Use when | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Default Wave A. You want facts and a clean ask | Least distinctive |
| **B** | The live page already carries the two questions | Reads like a think-piece if they do not know you |
| **C** | You still worry the list includes the wrong altitude | Can feel like a filter to a genuine Head of L&D |

Recommendation: **A for the posted wave.** Keep B as the email that follows delivery. Do not run A and B as two different handwritten bodies in the same post — they will compare.
