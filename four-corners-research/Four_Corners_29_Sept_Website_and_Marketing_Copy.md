## 2. Overview (the paragraph the page needs)

Most performance problems get labelled a skills gap. The next move is another learning programme. Completions go up. Monday does not change.

This afternoon is for the people who own that budget — in-house Heads of L&D, Talent and OD in organisations of about 250–5,000 — to test whether they have been diagnosing the wrong problem.

Charles Jennings asks why programmes fail as a solution system. Alex Boulting asks whether we are misdiagnosing performance problems as skills problems. Twenty minutes each. Then tables. We finish by naming a few “Corners” — themes this room actually wants to go deeper on — and those who want to continue meet later over coffee.

No exhibition. No supplier pitches. No attendee list handed out.

## 9. FAQ (replace the live “this event is free” answers)

**Who is it for?**  
In-house Heads of L&D, Talent and OD in organisations of about 250–5,000, London-commutable. Not a mixed seniority room.

**Is there a cost?**  
£400 + VAT. Invoice same week.

**Can I bring a colleague?**  
One person in a similar senior in-house role, if we have a place. Not a supplier and not an HRBP.

**Will there be suppliers?**  
No stands, no pitches, no 1-to-1s in the diary. Contributors may talk at canapés like anyone else. We do not hand them your details.

**Is this a training course?**  
No. You will not leave with a toolkit. You will leave having tested whether the last programme failed because it was the wrong diagnosis.

**Is this iVentiv / CIPD Festival of Work?**  
No. Those rooms are free, bigger, or a different altitude. This is a paid afternoon for people who own the UK learning budget in mid-sized organisations.

**Will I be put on the spot?**  
No. Contribution is in tables, as much or as little as you like.

**Where?**  
Chiswell Street Dining Rooms, EC1. Moorgate, Barbican or Liverpool Street.
