## 🚨 KEY WARNINGS FROM YOUR MENTOR

### 1. Don't Skip Validation
**The Trap:** "I already know the market needs this"  
**Reality:** 90% of event creators overestimate demand  
**Solution:** Do all 50 interviews before spending money

---

### 2. Don't Underestimate Costs
**The Trap:** "We can do this for €100k"  
**Reality:** First-time event organizers underbudget by 50%+  
**Solution:** Add 30% contingency to every line item

---

### 3. Don't Ignore Competition
**The Trap:** "We'll just be better"  
**Reality:** "Better" doesn't beat "different" + "established"  
**Solution:** Find structural differentiation, not incremental

---

### 4. Don't Launch Without Marketing Budget
**The Trap:** "Build it and they will come"  
**Reality:** You need €100k+ marketing for awareness  
**Solution:** 30% of total budget should be marketing

---

### 5. Don't Compromise on Quality Year 1
**The Trap:** "Year 1 is just a test"  
**Reality:** Bad Year 1 kills Year 2 before it starts  
**Solution:** Go big or go home (quality matters more than size)

## 🎓 MENTOR'S FINAL ADVICE

### The Three Questions That Matter:

**1. "What problem does this solve?"**
Not "we're better" but "we solve X pain for Y people"  
*If you can't answer this in one sentence, stop.*

**2. "Why me/us?"**
What unique advantage do you have? (Network, expertise, resources)  
*If answer is "passion" alone, reconsider.*

**3. "Why now?"**
What's changed in the market that makes this the right time?  
*If answer is "I want to do this," not enough.*

---

### The Honest Self-Assessment:

Answer these privately:

**Can I afford to lose €100k if this fails?**  
(Because you might)

**Can I commit 20+ hours/week for 12 months?**  
(Because you must)

**Can I handle public failure if this doesn't work?**  
(Because it's possible)

**Do I have support from family/partners for this intensity?**  
(Because it's draining)

**Am I doing this to solve a real problem or build my personal brand?**  
(Be honest - wrong reasons doom projects)

If you answered "no" to more than 2, seriously reconsider.
