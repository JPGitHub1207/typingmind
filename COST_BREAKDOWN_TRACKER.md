## Red Flags: When to Cut Costs

### 🚨 Stop spending if:

1. **No follower growth after 6 weeks**
   - Action: Pause paid tools, use only free tiers
   - Reassess content strategy

2. **Engagement rate < 1% after 8 weeks**
   - Action: Stop paid promotions
   - Focus on organic engagement

3. **Zero revenue after 12 weeks**
   - Action: Reduce subscriptions by 50%
   - Focus entirely on monetization

4. **Monthly costs > $300 with no revenue**
   - Action: Switch to minimal budget
   - Extend timeline

5. **Burning through savings**
   - Action: Pause, don't quit
   - Go completely free for 1-2 months

---

## When to INCREASE Investment

### 💰 Spend more when:

1. **Viral content emerging**
   - Invest $100-200 in promoting winning content
   - Boost posts that already have organic traction

2. **Engagement rate > 5%**
   - Your content resonates
   - Increase posting frequency
   - Invest in better tools

3. **First $500 revenue month**
   - Reinvest 50% back into business
   - Better tools, paid promotions

4. **Brand inquiries coming in**
   - Upgrade media kit
   - Invest in professional photos
   - Better website

5. **Hitting follower milestones fast**
   - You're ahead of plan
   - Accelerate content production
