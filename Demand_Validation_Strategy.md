# Demand Validation Strategy - Ethical Approach

## Your Situation
- 17 years in training business
- Industry knowledge and relationships
- Leaving current business
- Want to validate demand for new network
- Care about industry (not just quick buck)

## ⚠️ Why "Underhand" Approach is Risky

### Problems with Using Different Brand/Identity:

1. **Reputation Risk:**
   - If discovered, damages credibility
   - Industry is small - people talk
   - Could hurt your new business before it starts
   - Breaks trust with potential customers

2. **Legal/Ethical Issues:**
   - Misrepresentation
   - GDPR/data protection concerns
   - Could violate professional standards
   - May breach contracts (non-compete, confidentiality)

3. **Relationship Damage:**
   - People feel deceived
   - Hard to rebuild trust
   - Word spreads quickly in small industry
   - Could damage 17 years of relationships

4. **Not Necessary:**
   - You have legitimate ways to validate
   - Your experience gives you credibility
   - Transparency actually builds trust
   - People respect honesty

---

## ✅ ETHICAL VALIDATION STRATEGIES

### Strategy 1: Transparent Research (Recommended)

**Approach:**
- Be upfront about who you are and what you're doing
- Position as "industry research" or "exploring new opportunity"
- Leverage your 17 years of experience as credibility
- Ask for help/input, not sales

**Script Example:**
> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name]. After 17 years in the training industry, I'm exploring a new opportunity to support large levy employers with the Growth and Skills Levy changes coming in April 2026. I'm doing some research to understand if there's a real need. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation about the challenges you're facing?"

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Honest and transparent
- ✅ Leverages your credibility
- ✅ People respect research
- ✅ Builds relationships, doesn't damage them
- ✅ Gets you real information

**Questions to Ask:**
1. "Are you aware of the Growth and Skills Levy and apprenticeship units launching April 2026?"
2. "What are your biggest challenges with the current apprenticeship levy?"
3. "Would you consider becoming a provider to use your levy for units/short courses?"
4. "What support would you need if you were to do this?"
5. "Would you value a network of other large employers going through similar decisions?"
6. "What would make such a network valuable to you?"
7. "What would you be willing to pay for this kind of support?"

2. **Industry Contacts:**

**Script:**

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Uses existing relationships
- ✅ People want to help
- ✅ Gets honest feedback
- ✅ Builds support for launch
- ✅ Potential early customers

### Strategy 4: Free Pilot Event

**Approach:**
- Organize free event/webinar
- "Understanding the Growth and Skills Levy"
- Educational, not salesy
- Gauge interest and demand

**Event Format:**
1. **Content:**
   - Policy overview (Growth and Skills Levy)
   - Apprenticeship units explained
   - Requirements for employers
   - Q&A session

2. **Promotion:**
   - LinkedIn
   - Industry networks
   - Your existing contacts
   - AELP/industry bodies

3. **During Event:**
   - Educate, don't sell
   - Answer questions
   - Gauge interest
   - Ask: "Would you value ongoing support?"

4. **After Event:**
   - Follow up with attendees
   - Ask about interest in network

   - Get commitments

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Provides value (education)
- ✅ Builds credibility
- ✅ Tests demand
- ✅ Gets you in front of target market
- ✅ Creates early adopters

### Strategy 5: Advisory Board Approach

**Approach:**
- Recruit "advisory board" of potential customers
- "Help shape this new network"
- Get input on design, pricing, value
- Builds buy-in and early customers

**How to Do It:**
1. **Identify 5-10 Target Employers:**
   - Large levy payers
   - People you know or can reach
   - Industry leaders

2. **Invitation:**
   > "I'm developing a network to support large employers with Growth and Skills Levy. I'd love your input to make sure it's valuable. Would you join an advisory group? 2-3 meetings, your expertise would be invaluable."

3. **Benefits for Them:**
   - Shape something relevant
   - Early access
   - Discount/free membership
   - Thought leadership

4. **Benefits for You:**
   - Validates demand
   - Gets input on design
   - Tests pricing
   - Creates early customers
   - Builds credibility

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ People like being asked for input
   - ✅ Validates demand
   - ✅ Gets you customers
   - ✅ Builds relationships
   - ✅ Creates advocates
