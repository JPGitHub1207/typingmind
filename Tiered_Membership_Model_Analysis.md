# Tiered Membership Model Analysis

## Business Model Overview

### Three-Tier Structure:

**Entry: £100/month (£1,200/year)**
- 1 full day event per quarter (4/year)
- Monthly meetups
- 20% off training (2 half days per quarter)

**Member: £280/month (£3,360/year)**
- 2 places
- Rising star network (shadow, supported)
- All training included (free)
- 1 full day event
- Monthly meetups
- 2 online half days each month

**Growth: £420/month (£5,040/year)**
- 3 places
- All above included
- All training free
- 6 online half days (unlimited)
- What else?

**Upsell:** Training courses (additional revenue)

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES

### 1. PRICING IS TOO LOW - You're Undervaluing

**The Problem:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**Reality Check:**
- Corporate budgets can handle MUCH more
- You're competing with free (AELP, informal networks)
- Low price = low perceived value
- Hard to make money at these prices

**Comparison:**
- CIPD: £120-200/year (but that's for individuals, not corporates)
- Corporate training: £500-2,000 per day
- Your full day events cost you £5-10k to run
- At £100/month, you need 50-100 members to break even on ONE event

**Recommendation:** **Double or triple your prices**

### 2. UNIT ECONOMICS DON'T WORK

**Cost Per Event:**
- Venue: £3,000-8,000
- Catering: £2,000-4,000
- AV: £1,000-2,000
- Consultant/Speaker: £2,000-10,000
- Marketing: £500-1,000
- Your time: £2,000-5,000
- **Total: £10,500-30,000 per full day event**

**Revenue Per Event (Entry Tier):**
- 50 members × £100/month = £5,000/month
- But: That's for ALL benefits, not just one event
- Per event value: £5,000 / 4 events = £1,250 per event per member
- If 50 members attend: £62,500 revenue
- But: Costs = £10,500-30,000
- **Margin: £32,500-52,000 (if 50 members)**

**The Math Problem:**
- You need 20-30 Entry members to break even on ONE event
- But you're running 4 events + monthly meetups + training
- Plus your time
- **You'll lose money unless you get 50+ members quickly**

### 3. "CHEAP AND UPSELL" IS DANGEROUS

**The Risk:**
- Low entry price = attracts price-sensitive customers
- Price-sensitive = don't buy upsells
- Creates expectation of low prices
- Hard to raise prices later
- Attracts wrong customers

**Better Approach:**
- Higher entry price = attracts value-focused customers
- Value-focused = more likely to buy upsells
- Creates premium positioning
- Can add value, not just discount
- Attracts right customers

### 4. TRAINING AS UPSELL IS COMPETITIVE

**The Challenge:**
- Training market is saturated
- Lots of competitors
- Hard to differentiate
- Price pressure
- Quality expectations

**Questions:**
- What training will you offer?
- Who will deliver it?
- How much will it cost you?
- What's your margin?
- Why would they buy from you vs. others?

### 5. TIER STRUCTURE IS CONFUSING

**Issues:**
- "Entry" vs "Member" vs "Growth" - unclear progression
- What's the difference between "Member" and "Growth"?
- "Rising star network" - what is this?
- "Shadow, supported" - unclear value
- Too many variables (places, training, events)

**Recommendation:** Simplify tiers, clarify value

---

## 🟡 MODERATE CONCERNS

### 6. "RISING STAR NETWORK" - Unclear Value

**Questions:**
- What is this?
- Who are "rising stars"?
- What does "shadow, supported" mean?
- Is this valuable?
- Would people pay for it?

**Recommendation:** Clarify or remove

### 7. TRAINING INCLUDED - Cost Risk

**The Problem:**
- "All training included" = you pay for it
- Training costs money (consultants, content, delivery)
- If members use it heavily, you lose money
- Hard to predict usage

**Better Approach:**
- Training credits (e.g., 4 training sessions/year)
- Or: Training discount (not free)
- Or: Training as separate paid product

### 8. "PLACES" - Unclear Meaning

**Questions:**
- What are "places"?
- Places at events?
- Places in network?
- Places in training?
- Unclear value

**Recommendation:** Clarify or remove

## 🚨 CRITICAL RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. RAISE PRICES SIGNIFICANTLY

**Current:** £100-420/month
**Recommended:** £250-1,000/month

**Why:**
- Corporate budgets can handle it
- Higher perceived value
- Better unit economics
- Attracts right customers

**Why:**

- Clearer value proposition
- Better sales

### 3. CLARIFY VALUE PROPOSITION

**Current:** Unclear (rising star, shadow, places, etc.)
**Recommended:** Clear benefits (events, training, support)

**Why:**
- Easier to sell
- Clearer value
- Better positioning

### 4. RE-THINK "TRAINING INCLUDED"

**Current:** All training free (cost risk)
**Recommended:** Training credits or discounts

**Why:**
- Predictable costs
- Better unit economics
- Members value what they pay for

### 5. VALIDATE PRICING BEFORE LAUNCH

**Action:**
- Test pricing with 10 potential customers
- Ask: "Would you pay £X for this?"
- Test different price points
- Don't assume

**Why:**
- Don't price too low
- Don't price too high
- Find sweet spot
- Validate demand
