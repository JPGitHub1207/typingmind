## 🔍 Research Strategies

### 1. Local Authority Websites
Many provider networks are coordinated through local authority skills and employment teams.

**Search patterns:**
- `[Region] adult skills provider network`
- `[County Council] training provider forum`
- `[LEP name] skills provider network`

### 2. Combined Authorities & LEPs
Regional networks often operate under Combined Authorities or Local Enterprise Partnerships.

**Key Combined Authorities:**
- South Yorkshire MCA
- West Midlands CA
- Liverpool City Region CA

**LEP Areas:**
- D2N2 LEP
- Greater Lincolnshire LEP
- Marches LEP
- Solent LEP

### 3. Social Media & Professional Networks
- LinkedIn company pages
- Twitter/X accounts
- Facebook groups

### 4. Industry Contacts
- AELP regional forums
- Association of Colleges regional groups
- FE Week and other sector publications

### 5. Freedom of Information Requests
For networks associated with public bodies, FOI requests can reveal:
- Member lists
- Meeting minutes
- Contact information

### 6. Conference & Event Listings
Provider networks often host or participate in regional skills events.

## 💡 Tips

1. **Start with LEP websites** - Many networks are coordinated through Local Enterprise Partnerships
2. **Check Combined Authority skills pages** - Metropolitan areas often coordinate through CAs
3. **Search for meeting minutes** - Local authority websites may reference these networks
4. **Contact similar networks** - Organizations like HOLEX or AELP may have contact information
5. **Try archived websites** - Some networks may have closed or rebranded
