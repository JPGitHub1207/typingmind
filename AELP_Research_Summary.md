# AELP Workshop Providers Research - Executive Summary

## Research Overview
I have completed a comprehensive research project to identify organizations and consultants that deliver workshops for AELP (Association of Employment and Learning Providers). Here's what has been compiled:

## Key Deliverables Created

### 1. Main Research Report
**File:** `AELP_Workshop_Providers_Research.md`
- Complete analysis of 2025 conference workshop zones
- Detailed provider categorization
- Contact information and recommendations

### 2. Provider Database
**Files:** 
- `aelp_providers_database.csv` - Excel-compatible format with 55 providers
- `aelp_providers_database.json` - Machine-readable format
- Includes organization names, types, specializations, and likely 2025 workshop zones

### 3. Past Speakers Database
**File:** `aelp_past_speakers.csv`
- 22 documented past speakers from 2021-2024
- Includes names, roles, years, and session types

### 4. Research Tools
**Files:**
- `aelp_research.py` - Web research automation tool
- `aelp_providers_database.py` - Database generation tool
