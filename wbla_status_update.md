# Work Based Learning Alliance - Current Status

## ❌ NO - The Work Based Learning Alliance is NOT still going

Both registered companies have been **dissolved**:

### 1. **London Work Based Learning Alliance** (Company 05003899)
- **Status**: ❌ **DISSOLVED**
- **Dissolved on**: **30 April 2019**
- **Method**: Voluntary strike-off
- **Last confirmation statement**: 31 December 2018
- **Companies House**: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/05003899

### 2. **Work Based Learning Alliance Ltd** (Company 05983819)
- **Status**: ❌ **DISSOLVED**
- **Dissolved on**: **13 October 2020**
- **Method**: Voluntary strike-off
- **Last confirmation statement**: 31 October 2019
- **Companies House**: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/05983819

## What This Means:

Both organizations **voluntarily closed down** (strike-off), meaning:
- They chose to dissolve rather than being forced to close
- They are no longer operating as registered companies
- Any activities or networks they ran have ceased

## ✅ Active Alternatives:

Since the Work Based Learning Alliance is no longer active, the **similar networks that ARE still active** are:

### 1. **London Training Providers Network** ✅ ACTIVE
- **URL**: https://www.aelp.org.uk/membership/regional-network-membership/
- **Status**: Active (part of AELP)
- **Focus**: Training providers in London
- **Very similar** to the dissolved London WBLA

### 2. **London Apprenticeship Provider Network** ✅ ACTIVE
- **URL**: https://www.aelp.org.uk/membership/regional-network-membership/
- **Status**: Active (part of AELP)
- **Focus**: Apprenticeship providers in London
- **Very similar** to the dissolved London WBLA

### 3. **AELP (Association of Employment and Learning Providers)** ✅ ACTIVE
- **URL**: https://www.aelp.org.uk
- **Status**: Active
- **Focus**: Work-based learning providers nationwide
- **Description**: "AELP delivers a range of events, workshops, forums and webinars that bring together providers of work based learning provision across all sectors."

## Conclusion:

The Work Based Learning Alliance organizations **closed down in 2019-2020**. However, their functions and purpose are now served by:

1. **AELP's regional networks** (London Training Providers Network, London Apprenticeship Provider Network)
2. **AELP itself** as the main organization for work-based learning providers

These active alternatives serve the same purpose and target the same audience as the dissolved Work Based Learning Alliance.
