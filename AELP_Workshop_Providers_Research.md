## Research Strategy

### Primary Sources to Check:

1. **AELP Website (www.aelp.org.uk)**
   - Events page
   - Past events archive
   - News/announcements section
   - Speaker/partner pages

2. **AELP National Conference Pages**
   - Conference programs (current and archived)
   - Speaker biographies
   - Workshop session details
   - Exhibitor lists

3. **Social Media & Professional Networks**
   - LinkedIn: Search for "AELP speaker" or "AELP workshop"
   - Twitter/X: #AELP hashtags, @AELP_UK
   - Conference attendee posts and photos

4. **Registration/Booking Platforms**
   - Eventbrite, Hopin, or custom AELP event platform
   - May contain speaker information

5. **AELP Member Organizations**
   - Training providers who are AELP members
   - Regular contributors to AELP events

### Secondary Sources:

6. **Industry Publications**
   - FE News (Further Education)
   - FE Week
   - Skills Builder publications

7. **Government Departments**
   - Department for Education (DfE)
   - Ofsted
   - NHS Learning & Development

8. **Known Training Consultancies**
   - Organizations specializing in apprenticeships
   - FE sector consultancies
   - EdTech companies

## Questions to Help Focus the Search

1. **Timeframe:** How far back should I search for historical events?
   - Last 1 year? 3 years? 5 years?

2. **Event Types:** Which types of events are most important?
   - National conferences only?
   - Regional events?
   - Online webinars?
   - All of the above?

3. **Information Format:** What details do you need for each provider/consultant?
   - Name of organization/individual
   - Workshop topics they've covered
   - Contact information
   - Frequency of participation
   - Areas of expertise

4. **Purpose:** What will you use this information for?
   - To contact them for future workshops?
   - To understand AELP's network?
   - For competitive analysis?
   - For partnership opportunities?

5. **Specific Interests:** Are there specific topic areas you're most interested in?
   - Apprenticeships
   - Technology/Digital
   - Policy/Compliance
   - Teaching & Learning
   - Employer engagement
   - All topics?

## Next Steps

1. **Check AELP Event Confirmation Emails**
   - You may have received booking confirmations with speaker names
   - Look for workshop descriptor PDFs

2. **Contact AELP Directly**
   - Email: info@aelp.org.uk or events@aelp.org.uk
   - Request the full conference program with speaker names
   - Ask for access to previous event programs

3. **Check Your AELP Account**
   - Log into the AELP member portal
   - Event booking history may have detailed programs

4. **Network with Other Attendees**
   - AELP LinkedIn group members
   - Ask who spoke at sessions they attended

5. **Web Archive Search**
   - Use Wayback Machine (archive.org) to find old AELP event pages
   - May contain speaker information that's no longer live
