## Appendix: Workshop Selection Process

When registering for AELP conferences, delegates typically:
1. Choose from concurrent workshop sessions
2. Select based on relevance to their role and organization
3. Receive confirmation with workshop allocations
4. May request changes subject to availability

Workshop providers are usually confirmed 6-8 weeks before the event, with full details released 2-4 weeks prior through the conference app and delegate packs.
