## Success Tips

1. **Start with AELP directly** - They're your best source
2. **Check email first** - You may already have the information
3. **Use specific search terms** - "Digital badging AELP" better than just "AELP"
4. **Try multiple platforms** - LinkedIn, Twitter, website all have different info
5. **Look at images** - Conference photos often show name badges
6. **Check hashtags** - Live conference tweets reveal speakers
7. **Network** - Other attendees may remember past speakers
8. **Be persistent** - Follow up if no response
9. **Document everything** - Use the CSV template provided
10. **Cross-reference** - Verify names across multiple sources
