# Verification Report: FE Provider Networks vs Training Provider Networks

## Important Distinction

**Further Education (FE) Colleges** = Public institutions like:
- Capital City College Group
- New City College  
- London South East Colleges
- etc.

**Training Providers** = Private companies delivering apprenticeships/training contracts

---

## Analysis of Requested Networks

### 1. London Skills for Growth
- **URL:** https://www.londonskillsforgrowth.co.uk
- **Description:** "Network of training providers supporting skills development in London"
- **Status:** ⚠️ **LIKELY NOT** an FE provider network
- **Reason:** Described as "training providers" not "FE colleges"
- **Verdict:** This appears to be for independent training providers, not FE college directors/staff

### 2. London Learning Consortium  
- **URL:** https://www.londonlearningconsortium.co.uk
- **Description:** "Consortium of training providers in London"
- **Status:** ⚠️ **LIKELY NOT** an FE provider network
- **Reason:** Explicitly described as "training providers" not "FE colleges"
- **Verdict:** This is for training providers, not FE college directors/staff

### 3. London Providers Network
- **URL:** Not available
- **Description:** "Network of independent training providers in London"
- **Status:** ❌ **NOT** an FE provider network
- **Reason:** Explicitly states "independent training providers"
- **Verdict:** This is definitely for training providers, not FE colleges

---

## Actual FE Provider Networks

### ✅ Association of Colleges (AoC) London Region
- **URL:** https://www.aoc.co.uk
- **Type:** Network/Association
- **Description:** Regional network representing further education colleges in London
- **Status:** ✅ **YES - This IS an FE provider network**
- **Members:** FE college principals, directors, and staff from London's FE colleges
- **Purpose:** Where FE college leaders from different organizations come together

### ✅ Greater London Authority Skills for Londoners
- **URL:** https://www.london.gov.uk/what-we-do/skills-and-employment
- **Type:** Partnership Network
- **Description:** Strategic partnership including FE colleges, GLA, and training providers
- **Status:** ✅ **YES - Includes FE colleges** (but also other stakeholders)
- **Members:** FE colleges participate alongside other organizations

---

## Conclusion

**The three networks you asked about (London Skills for Growth, London Learning Consortium, London Providers Network) appear to be for TRAINING PROVIDERS, not Further Education colleges.**

**For networks where FE college directors/staff from different FE organizations come together, the primary network is:**

### Association of Colleges (AoC) London Region
**Website:** https://www.aoc.co.uk

This is the main professional network where principals, directors, and senior staff from London's Further Education colleges meet, collaborate, and share best practices.

---

## Recommendation

If you need networks specifically for FE college directors/staff, focus on:
1. **Association of Colleges (AoC) London Region** - Primary network
2. **Greater London Authority Skills for Londoners** - Partnership including FE colleges
3. Individual college groups may have their own networks (e.g., Capital City College Group has internal networks)

The three networks you asked about should be removed or reclassified as "Training Provider Networks" rather than "FE Provider Networks" if you're specifically looking for networks where FE college directors/staff meet.
