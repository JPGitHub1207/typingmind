Gateshead Council Learning and Skills one of the North East’s leading providers for post-16 education, second largest local authority provider of apprenticeships in the UK who successfully engage with employers to deliver high quality training.

A team of vocational experts specialising in a range of programmes at all levels will work with you to design a training solution that meets your needs to ensure your apprentice has the skills and knowledge to contribute to your organisation’s success. Strong pastoral support to enhance the personal and professional development of your apprentice, giving the necessary guidance and resources to thrive in their role and ensure a successful outcome.

In September 2024 Ofsted rated us as a ‘good’ provider.
