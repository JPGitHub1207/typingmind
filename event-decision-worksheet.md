## SECTION 4: Audience Validation Checklist

### Pre-Launch Conversations Required

**Target: 50 conversations** (10 per segment)

| Segment | Target # | Completed | Validation Score* |
|---------|----------|-----------|------------------|
| Established Companies | 10 | ___ | ___/10 |
| Startups | 10 | ___ | ___/10 |
| Investors | 10 | ___ | ___/10 |
| Government/Agencies | 10 | ___ | ___/10 |
| Service Providers | 10 | ___ | ___/10 |
| **TOTAL** | **50** | **___** | **___/10** |

*Validation Score: Average of "Would you pay €500 to attend?" (1=No, 10=Definitely)

**Key Questions to Ask:**

1. What space tech events do you currently attend?
   - *Look for: Dissatisfaction, unmet needs*

2. What would make you choose a new event over established ones?
   - *Look for: Specific pain points you can solve*

3. What's the maximum you'd pay for [describe your event]?
   - *Look for: Price sensitivity, willingness to pay*

4. Who would you want to meet there?
   - *Look for: Network value expectations*

5. What would make this event a "must-attend" for you?
   - *Look for: Critical success factors*

**Go/No-Go Criteria:**
- ✅ 30+ people score 7+ on validation
- ✅ Consistent patterns in needs/pain points
- ✅ At least 3 segments show strong interest
