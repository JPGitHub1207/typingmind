# Questions to Ask AELP About Workshop Presenters

## Email Template

**Subject:** Request for Workshop Presenter Information - 2025 National Conference

**To:** [AELP Events Team - find email on contact page]

**Message:**

Dear AELP Events Team,

I am registered for the 2025 National Conference and would like to obtain information about the organizations and consultants delivering the workshops. I have selected the following workshops but cannot find the presenter information:

1. **EMPLOYER ZONE** - "How the NHS are navigating the current skills landscape"
2. **FUNDING ZONE** - "DfE / Apprenticeship Service – Improving access to funding"
3. **GEN Z ZONE** - "Empowering Gen Z: how to deliver high quality Careers Education"
4. **GROWTH ZONE** - "Insight into Action: SME engagement in skills provision"
5. **INNOVATION ZONE** - "Future-Proofing Provision: Retain, Support, and Succeed with Predictive Data & Analytics"
6. **PARTNERSHIP & COLLABORATION ZONE** - "Navigating Change: Exploring the Future of Apprenticeships"
7. **POLICY ZONE** - "Ask the Inspector"
8. **TEACHING & LEARNING ZONE** - "Bridging the Skills Gap: Apprenticeships & CPD Working Together"
9. **TECHNOLOGY ZONE** - "A Practical Introduction to the Impact of Digital Badging for ITPs"

Could you please provide:
- The names of the organizations/consultants delivering each workshop
- Speaker names and bios (if available)
- A full conference program with presenter information
- Information about previous conference workshop facilitators (for reference)

I am also interested in learning about workshop presenters from previous AELP events to understand the types of organizations and consultants that typically deliver workshops.

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Organization]
[Your Contact Information]

## Phone Call Talking Points

1. **Introduction:** "I'm registered for the 2025 conference and need information about workshop presenters."

2. **Specific Request:** "I've selected workshops but can't find who's delivering them. Can you provide presenter information?"

3. **Additional Questions:**
   - "When will the full conference program with speaker names be available?"
   - "Is there a conference app or portal where this information will be posted?"
   - "Can you share information about previous conference workshop facilitators?"
   - "Who should I contact if I want to connect with workshop presenters before the conference?"

4. **Follow-up:** "Is there an email I can send this request to for a written response?"

## What Information to Request

✅ **Essential Information:**
- Organization/company name for each workshop
- Presenter names
- Presenter job titles/roles
- Brief bios (if available)

✅ **Helpful Additional Information:**
- Contact details for presenters (if appropriate)
- Previous conference presenter lists
- Pattern of workshop facilitators across years
- Sponsor/exhibitor information (as they often present)

✅ **Timeline Questions:**
- When will full program be released?
- When will speaker bios be available?
- Will there be a conference app with this information?
