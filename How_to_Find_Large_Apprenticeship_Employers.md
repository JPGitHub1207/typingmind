# How to Find Large Employers That Engage with Apprenticeships

## Research Objective
Find a comprehensive list of large employers (levy payers) that currently engage with apprenticeships to target for your network.

---

## 🎯 METHOD 1: Government/Public Sources (Free)

### 1.1 Apprenticeship Service Data (DfE)

**Source:** Department for Education / Apprenticeship Service
**Access:** Public data, may require FOI request

**What's Available:**
- Employer apprenticeship data
- Levy payer information
- Apprenticeship starts by employer
- Funding data

**How to Access:**
1. **Check DfE Website:**
   - www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-education
   - Look for "Apprenticeship statistics"
   - "Apprenticeship data"

2. **Apprenticeship Service:**
   - Check if they publish employer lists
   - May have public dashboards
   - Data releases

3. **FOI Request:**
   - Freedom of Information request to DfE
   - Request list of levy-paying employers
   - Request apprenticeship starts by employer
   - May take 20 working days

**FOI Request Template:**
> "I would like to request the following information under the Freedom of Information Act:
> 
> 1. A list of all employers who pay the apprenticeship levy (annual pay bill over £3 million)
> 2. For each employer, the number of apprenticeship starts in the last 12 months
> 3. The total amount of levy funds used by each employer in the last 12 months
> 
> If this data is not available in the exact format requested, please provide what is available."

**Contact:**
- DfE FOI Team: foi.requests@education.gov.uk
- Or use: www.gov.uk/make-a-freedom-of-information-request

### 1.2 Skills England / Apprenticeship Service

**Source:** Skills England (new body)
**Access:** Check their website for data

**Website:**
- skillsengland.education.gov.uk
- May have employer data
- Apprenticeship standards data
- Provider data

### 1.3 HMRC Data (Levy Payers)

**Source:** HMRC (collects the levy)
**Access:** May require FOI request

**What's Available:**
- List of levy-paying employers
- Annual pay bill data
- Levy amounts paid

**FOI Request:**
> "I would like to request a list of all employers who paid the apprenticeship levy in the last financial year, including their company name and approximate levy amount."

**Contact:**
- HMRC FOI: foi.requests@hmrc.gsi.gov.uk

### 1.4 Government Statistics

**Source:** ONS, DfE Statistics
**Access:** Public data

**Where to Look:**
- www.gov.uk/government/statistics
- Search "apprenticeship"
- "Apprenticeship starts by employer"
- "Levy data"

## 🎯 METHOD 4: Research Methods

### 4.1 Web Scraping / Research

**Approach:** Manual research
**Time:** Labor-intensive but free

**How to Do It:**
1. **Start with Known Lists:**
   - FTSE 100 companies
   - FTSE 250 companies
   - Large private companies
   - Check each for apprenticeship programs

2. **Check Company Websites:**
   - Careers pages
   - "Apprenticeships" sections
   - News/press releases
   - "Our people" sections

3. **Google Searches:**
   - "[Company name] apprenticeships"
   - "[Company name] apprenticeship program"
   - "[Company name] levy"

4. **LinkedIn Research:**
   - Search company + "apprenticeship"
   - Check if they have apprenticeship managers
   - Look for apprenticeship posts

5. **Build Spreadsheet:**
   - Company name
   - Industry
   - Size (employees)
   - Apprenticeship program (yes/no)
   - Contact (L&D Manager, Apprenticeship Manager)
   - Source

### 4.2 Industry Sector Research

**Approach:** Research by industry
**Focus:** Sectors known for apprenticeships

**High Apprenticeship Sectors:**
- Engineering/Manufacturing
- Construction
- Retail
- Hospitality
- Healthcare
- Financial Services
- Technology
- Public Sector

**How to Do It:**
1. **Identify Top Companies in Each Sector:**
   - Industry rankings
   - Sector reports
   - Trade associations

2. **Check Each for Apprenticeships:**
   - Website research
   - LinkedIn research
   - News articles

3. **Build Sector Lists:**
   - Engineering companies with apprenticeships
   - Retail companies with apprenticeships
   - etc.

### 4.3 Regional Research

**Approach:** Research by region
**Focus:** Large employers in each region

**How to Do It:**
1. **Identify Regional Large Employers:**
   - Regional business directories

2. **Check for Apprenticeships:**

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### 1. Start with FOI (Free, Official)
- Most comprehensive
- Official data
- Free
- Just takes time (20 days)

### 2. Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Best for finding contacts
- Advanced filters
- Export capabilities
- Worth the £60/month if you're serious

### 3. Build as You Go
- Don't wait for perfect list
- Start with what you have
- Add as you find more
- Iterate

### 4. Verify Before Outreach
- Check company actually has apprenticeships
- Find right contact (L&D/Apprenticeship Manager)
- Personalize outreach
- Higher response rate

### 5. Track Your Sources
- Know where each company came from
- Helps prioritize
- Understand your market
- Refine approach
