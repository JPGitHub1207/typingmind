## Recommended Workflow

### Step 1: Understand the Landscape (Week 1-2)
1. Read **EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md**
2. Review **COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md**
3. Review **OPPORTUNITIES.md**
4. Get familiar with the market

### Step 2: Conduct Research (Week 3-8)
1. Use **MARKET_RESEARCH_FRAMEWORK.md** to plan research
2. Use **RESEARCH_DATA_COLLECTION.md** templates to collect data
3. Conduct interviews and surveys
4. Build your databases

### Step 3: Analyze Your Position (Week 9-10)
1. Complete **SWOT_ANALYSIS.md**
2. Review research findings
3. Refine your event concept
4. Validate with stakeholders

### Step 4: Make Decisions (Week 11+)
1. Review **EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md** action plan
2. Make go/no-go decision
3. If go: Begin detailed planning
4. If no-go: Pivot or delay

## Key Principles

### 1. Be Critical
- Challenge your assumptions
- Seek negative feedback
- Don't fall in love with your idea

### 2. Validate First
- Research before building
- Interview before committing
- Test before scaling

### 3. Be Different
- Don't copy competitors
- Find your unique angle
- Solve a real problem

### 4. Start Small
- Prove concept first
- Learn and iterate
- Scale after validation

### 5. Execute Well
- Details matter
- Follow through
- Build credibility

## Critical Questions Checklist

Before proceeding, ensure you can answer:

- [ ] What's your unique value proposition? (One sentence)
- [ ] Who is your target audience? (Be specific)
- [ ] Why will sponsors pay? (What's their ROI?)
- [ ] What's your unfair advantage?
- [ ] What's your minimum viable event?
- [ ] What's your break-even point?
- [ ] What's your biggest risk?
- [ ] How will you mitigate that risk?

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Assuming you know what people want** - Research first
❌ **Copying competitors** - Be different
❌ **Starting too big** - Start small, scale up
❌ **Ignoring weaknesses** - Address them honestly
❌ **Underestimating costs** - Events are expensive
❌ **Overestimating demand** - Validate first
❌ **Poor timing** - Check the calendar
❌ **Weak differentiation** - Must be clear

## Success Indicators

### Green Lights (Proceed)
- ✅ Clear differentiation from competitors
- ✅ Validated market demand
- ✅ Strong value proposition
- ✅ Realistic financial model
- ✅ Key resources secured
- ✅ Manageable risks

### Red Lights (Stop/Pivot)
- ❌ No clear differentiation
- ❌ No validated demand
- ❌ Weak value proposition
- ❌ Unrealistic financials
- ❌ Can't secure resources
- ❌ Unmanageable risks
