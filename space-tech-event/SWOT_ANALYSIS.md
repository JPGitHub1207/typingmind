## Critical Assessment Questions

### After completing your SWOT, answer these:

1. **Do your strengths align with key opportunities?**
   - If NO → You may be pursuing the wrong opportunity
   - If YES → This is your focus area

2. **Can you address critical weaknesses before launch?**
   - If NO → Consider delaying or pivoting
   - If YES → Create action plan

3. **Are threats manageable?**
   - If NO → Reconsider the concept
   - If YES → Develop mitigation strategies

4. **Is there a clear path to success?**
   - Based on SWOT, what's your winning strategy?
   - Is it realistic?

5. **What's your biggest risk?**
   - Identify the single biggest threat
   - How will you address it?

## SWOT-Based Decision Framework

### Green Light (Proceed)
- ✅ Strengths align with opportunities
- ✅ Weaknesses are addressable
- ✅ Threats are manageable
- ✅ Clear competitive advantage
- ✅ Market demand validated

### Yellow Light (Proceed with Caution)
- ⚠️ Some alignment but gaps exist
- ⚠️ Weaknesses need significant work
- ⚠️ Threats are real but manageable
- ⚠️ Competitive advantage unclear
- ⚠️ Market demand uncertain

### Red Light (Don't Proceed / Pivot)
- ❌ Strengths don't match opportunities
- ❌ Critical weaknesses can't be addressed
- ❌ Threats are too severe
- ❌ No clear competitive advantage
- ❌ Market demand not validated
