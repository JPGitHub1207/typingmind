# Market Research Framework for European Space Tech Event

## Research Objectives

1. Validate market demand for your event concept
2. Understand attendee needs and pain points
3. Identify pricing sensitivity
4. Map the competitive landscape
5. Identify key stakeholders and influencers
6. Understand sponsor motivations and budgets

---

## Research Methodology

### Phase 1: Secondary Research (Desk Research)

#### 1.1 Competitor Event Analysis
**What to research:**
- Event websites and marketing materials
- Social media presence and engagement
- Press releases and media coverage
- Pricing strategies
- Speaker lineups
- Sponsor lists
- Attendance numbers (if available)
- Post-event reports and feedback

**Tools:**
- Google searches
- LinkedIn event pages
- Event industry databases (Eventbrite, Meetup, etc.)
- Industry publications
- Social media monitoring

**Deliverable:** Competitor database with key metrics

---

#### 1.2 Industry Landscape Mapping
**What to research:**
- European space tech companies (by size, stage, focus)
- Investors active in space tech
- Government agencies and programs
- Industry associations
- Media and publications
- Key opinion leaders and influencers

**Tools:**
- Crunchbase, PitchBook (for companies/investors)
- ESA, national space agency websites
- Industry association directories
- LinkedIn searches
- Google Scholar (for academic connections)

**Deliverable:** Ecosystem map with key stakeholders

---

#### 1.3 Market Trends Analysis
**What to research:**
- Funding trends in European space tech
- Regulatory changes
- Technology trends
- Market growth projections
- Government initiatives

**Tools:**
- Industry reports (Euroconsult, NSR, etc.)
- Government policy documents
- Industry news aggregators
- Research papers

**Deliverable:** Market trends report

---

### Phase 2: Primary Research (Direct Outreach)

#### 2.1 Stakeholder Interviews

**Target Groups:**
1. **Potential Attendees**
   - Space tech startup founders/CEOs
   - Corporate space tech executives
   - Investors
   - Government/agency representatives
   - Academics and researchers

2. **Potential Sponsors**
   - Large space companies
   - Service providers (legal, consulting, etc.)
   - Technology companies
   - Government agencies

3. **Industry Experts**
   - Event organizers (other industries)
   - Industry association leaders
   - Media representatives

**Interview Questions Template:**

**For Potential Attendees:**
1. What space tech events do you currently attend? Why?
2. What's missing from current events?
3. What would make you attend a new event?
4. What's your budget for events annually?
5. What format do you prefer? (Conference, expo, networking, etc.)
6. What topics are most important to you?
7. Who would you want to meet at an event?
8. What would make an event "must-attend" vs. "nice-to-have"?

**For Potential Sponsors:**
1. What events do you sponsor? Why?
2. What ROI do you expect from event sponsorship?
3. What's your typical sponsorship budget?
4. What do you look for in an event to sponsor?
5. What sponsorship packages work best for you?
6. What metrics matter? (Attendee quality, quantity, leads, brand awareness)

**For Industry Experts:**
1. What's your assessment of the current event landscape?
2. What gaps do you see?
3. What would make a new event successful?
4. Who are the key stakeholders to engage?
5. What are common mistakes new event organizers make?

**Deliverable:** Interview transcripts and insights summary

---

#### 2.2 Surveys

**Target Audiences:**
- Space tech professionals (LinkedIn, industry groups)
- Previous event attendees (if you can access lists)
- Industry association members

**Survey Structure:**
1. Demographics (role, company, location)
2. Current event attendance patterns
3. Event preferences (format, topics, location, timing)
4. Pricing sensitivity
5. Pain points with current events
6. Interest in new event concepts
7. Willingness to pay

**Tools:**
- SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Google Forms
- LinkedIn polls
- Industry forums

**Deliverable:** Survey results with statistical analysis

---

#### 2.3 Focus Groups

**Format:**
- Small groups (6-8 people)
- 90-minute sessions
- Moderated discussion
- Test event concepts and formats

**Topics:**
- Event concept validation
- Format preferences
- Pricing discussions
- Content priorities
- Networking preferences

**Deliverable:** Focus group report with recommendations

## Key Metrics to Track

### Market Size Indicators
- Total number of space tech companies in Europe
- Total funding raised (annual)
- Number of active investors
- Government spending on space programs
- Number of events and total attendance

### Competitive Metrics
- Number of competing events
- Average attendance per event
- Average ticket prices
- Sponsor counts and values
- Market share (if calculable)

### Demand Indicators
- Survey response rates
- Interview interest levels
- Social media engagement on space tech topics
- Industry association membership growth
- Job postings in space tech

## Critical Research Questions

### Market Validation
1. Is there demand for another space tech event?
2. What specific need would your event fill?
3. Who would attend and why?
4. What would they pay?
5. When would they attend?

### Competitive Positioning
1. How are you different from competitors?
2. What's your unique value proposition?
3. Can you compete on price, quality, or both?
4. What's your sustainable competitive advantage?

### Feasibility
1. Can you attract quality speakers?
2. Can you secure sponsors?
3. Do you have the resources to execute?
4. What's the minimum viable event?
5. What's the break-even point?

## Common Research Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Not validating assumptions** - Don't assume you know what people want
❌ **Too small sample size** - Need 20+ interviews minimum
❌ **Leading questions** - Ask open-ended questions
❌ **Ignoring negative feedback** - Critical feedback is most valuable
❌ **Not researching competitors thoroughly** - You'll repeat their mistakes
❌ **Focusing only on attendees** - Sponsors are equally important
❌ **Not tracking metrics** - You need data to make decisions
❌ **Research paralysis** - At some point, you need to act
