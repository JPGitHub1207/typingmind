# Event Validation Framework

**Critical Principle**: Don't build an event until you've validated demand. This framework helps you test your assumptions before investing significant time and money.

---

## Phase 1: Problem Validation (Week 1-2)

### Goal: Confirm there's a real problem/need

### Questions to Answer:

1. **What problem are you solving?**
   - [ ] Write down the specific problem in one sentence
   - [ ] Is this problem felt by enough people to justify an event?

2. **Who has this problem?**
   - [ ] Define your target persona(s)
   - [ ] How many of them exist? (Total addressable market)

3. **How are they solving it today?**
   - [ ] What alternatives exist?
   - [ ] Why aren't those alternatives sufficient?

### Validation Methods:

#### Method 1: Survey (Target: 50-100 responses)
**Questions to ask:**

1. How often do you attend space tech events?
   - [ ] Never
   - [ ] 1-2 per year
   - [ ] 3-5 per year
   - [ ] 6+ per year

2. What's your biggest frustration with current space tech events?
   - [Open text]

3. What would make you more likely to attend a space tech event?
   - [Open text]

4. What's the ideal format for you?
   - [ ] 1-day conference
   - [ ] 2-day conference
   - [ ] Multi-day workshop
   - [ ] Virtual
   - [ ] Hybrid

5. What topics are most important to you?
   - [Checkboxes: List 10-15 topics]

6. What's your budget for attending events?
   - [ ] €0-€500
   - [ ] €500-€1,000
   - [ ] €1,000-€2,500
   - [ ] €2,500+

7. Would you be interested in [YOUR EVENT CONCEPT]?
   - [ ] Definitely yes
   - [ ] Probably yes
   - [ ] Maybe
   - [ ] Probably no
   - [ ] Definitely no

8. What would make you say "definitely yes"?
   - [Open text]

**Distribution Channels:**
- LinkedIn (space tech groups)
- Email lists (if you have them)
- Industry forums
- Direct outreach to target personas

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ 30%+ say "definitely yes" or "probably yes"
- ✅ Clear patterns in frustrations/needs
- ✅ Budget aligns with your pricing assumptions

---

#### Method 2: Interviews (Target: 10-15 deep conversations)

**Who to interview:**
- 5-7 potential attendees (your target personas)
- 3-5 potential sponsors
- 2-3 industry experts/advisors

**Interview Script:**

**For Attendees:**
1. Tell me about the last space tech event you attended.
   - What did you like?
   - What didn't work?
   - What was missing?

2. What would make an event a "must-attend" for you?
   - Content? Networking? Speakers? Format?

3. How do you decide which events to attend?
   - What factors matter most?

4. What's your budget for events?
   - Who pays? (Company vs. personal)

5. If I created [YOUR EVENT CONCEPT], would you attend?
   - What would need to be true for you to say yes?
   - What would make you say no?

6. Who else should I talk to? (Get referrals)

**For Sponsors:**
1. What events do you currently sponsor?
   - Why those events?
   - What ROI do you measure?

2. What makes a sponsorship valuable to you?
   - Leads? Brand awareness? Thought leadership?

3. What's your typical sponsorship budget?
   - How do you decide?

4. What would make you sponsor a new event?
   - What red flags would make you say no?

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ At least 60% express genuine interest
- ✅ Clear value proposition emerges
- ✅ Pricing feedback aligns with your model
- ✅ Get 2-3 referrals from each interview

## Phase 2: Solution Validation (Week 3-4)

### Goal: Test if your specific event concept resonates

### Create a "Landing Page" (No-Code Options: Carrd, Webflow, Framer)

**Elements to include:**
- Event name and tagline
- Value proposition (what problem it solves)
- Target audience
- Key topics/speakers (even if TBD)
- Date/location (even if tentative)
- Pricing tiers
- "Reserve Your Spot" or "Get Notified" button

**Don't build the full event yet!** This is just to test interest.

### Metrics to Track:

1. **Traffic**: How many people visit?
   - Target: 500-1,000 visitors in 2 weeks

2. **Conversion Rate**: How many sign up?
   - Target: 5-10% conversion (25-100 signups)

3. **Source**: Where did they come from?
   - Helps validate marketing channels

4. **Feedback**: Ask why they're interested
   - What resonated? What's missing?

### Pre-Sell Test (Week 4-5)

**Critical Test**: Try to pre-sell 20-30 tickets before announcing the event.

**Offer:**
- Early bird pricing (30-40% discount)
- "Founding Member" status
- Money-back guarantee if event doesn't happen

**Success Criteria:**
- ✅ 20-30 paid commitments
- ✅ This proves real demand, not just interest

**If you can't pre-sell 20 tickets:**
- ⚠️ Your concept may not be strong enough
- ⚠️ Revisit problem validation
- ⚠️ Adjust value proposition

---

## Phase 3: Sponsor Validation (Week 5-6)

### Goal: Get 2-3 anchor sponsors committed before launch

### Create a Sponsor Prospectus

**Include:**
- Event concept and value proposition
- Target audience demographics
- Expected attendance
- Sponsorship packages and pricing
- What's included in each package
- Why this event is different/valuable

### Outreach Strategy:

1. **List 20-30 potential sponsors**
   - Companies that sponsor similar events
   - Companies targeting your audience
   - Companies in your ecosystem

2. **Warm introductions** (preferred)
   - Use your network
   - Ask advisors for introductions

3. **Cold outreach** (if needed)
   - Personalized emails
   - LinkedIn messages
   - Follow up consistently

### Success Criteria:

- ✅ 2-3 anchor sponsors committed (€10K-€25K each)
- ✅ Covers 30-50% of event costs
- ✅ Reduces financial risk

**If you can't get sponsors:**
- ⚠️ Event concept may not be compelling
- ⚠️ Pricing may be too high
- ⚠️ Need stronger value proposition

---

## Phase 4: Advisory Board Validation (Week 6-7)

### Goal: Recruit 3-5 industry veterans to lend credibility

### Why You Need an Advisory Board:

- Credibility (helps with speakers, sponsors, attendees)
- Connections (introductions to speakers/sponsors)
- Content guidance (what topics matter?)
- Marketing (they'll promote to their networks)

### Who to Recruit:

- Former executives from space companies
- Well-known investors in space tech
- Industry thought leaders
- Former government/ESA officials

### What to Offer:

- Free tickets
- Speaking opportunity (if desired)
- Recognition (advisory board listing)
- Input on event direction

### Success Criteria:

- ✅ 3-5 advisors committed
- ✅ At least 2 are "name brand" recognizable
- ✅ They're actively helping (not just lending name)

## Phase 5: Go/No-Go Decision (Week 8)

### Decision Matrix

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-10) | Weighted Score |
|----------|--------|--------------|----------------|
| Problem validated (survey/interviews) | 20% | ___ | ___ |
| Solution validated (landing page signups) | 15% | ___ | ___ |
| Pre-sold tickets (20-30) | 25% | ___ | ___ |
| Anchor sponsors (2-3) | 20% | ___ | ___ |
| Advisory board (3-5) | 10% | ___ | ___ |
| Personal readiness (time/resources) | 10% | ___ | ___ |
| **TOTAL** | **100%** | | **___/10** |

### Go Criteria:

- ✅ Total score ≥ 7.0
- ✅ Pre-sold at least 15 tickets
- ✅ At least 1 anchor sponsor committed
- ✅ At least 2 advisors committed
- ✅ You have time/resources to execute

### No-Go Criteria:

- ❌ Total score < 6.0
- ❌ Can't pre-sell tickets
- ❌ No sponsor interest
- ❌ No advisor support
- ❌ Insufficient resources

**If No-Go:**
- Don't give up! Pivot based on learnings
- Revisit problem validation
- Adjust concept and re-validate

## Validation Checklist

Before launching your event, confirm:

- [ ] Surveyed 50+ target attendees
- [ ] Interviewed 10+ potential customers
- [ ] Created landing page and got 25+ signups
- [ ] Pre-sold 20-30 tickets
- [ ] Got 2-3 anchor sponsors committed
- [ ] Recruited 3-5 advisory board members
- [ ] Validated pricing with target customers
- [ ] Confirmed date/location works for target audience
- [ ] Identified 5-10 potential speakers (even if not confirmed)
- [ ] Created detailed budget and financial model
- [ ] Have go-to-market plan
- [ ] Have contingency plan if things go wrong

## Common Validation Mistakes

### ❌ Don't Do This:

1. **Skip validation** - "I know people want this"
2. **Ask friends/family** - They'll say yes to be nice
3. **Assume demand** - "There are 500 companies, so 200 will attend"
4. **Ignore negative feedback** - Confirmation bias
5. **Validate too late** - After you've already invested

### ✅ Do This Instead:

1. **Validate early** - Before building anything
2. **Talk to strangers** - They'll be honest
3. **Get paid commitments** - Pre-sales prove real demand
4. **Listen to "no"** - Understand why people say no
5. **Iterate based on feedback** - Pivot if needed

---

## Next Steps After Validation

If validation is successful:

1. **Finalize event concept** based on feedback
2. **Set date and secure venue** (or virtual platform)
3. **Build full event website**
4. **Open general ticket sales**
5. **Recruit speakers** (use advisory board connections)
6. **Finalize sponsor packages**
7. **Create marketing plan**
8. **Execute!**

---

**Remember**: Validation is not about proving you're right. It's about finding out if you're wrong BEFORE you invest significant time and money. Be honest with yourself. If validation fails, that's valuable information. Pivot and try again.
