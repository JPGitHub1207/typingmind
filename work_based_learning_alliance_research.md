# Work Based Learning Alliance - Research and Comparison

## Overview

The Work Based Learning Alliance (WBLA) is a network/organization focused on work-based learning, apprenticeships, and training provision. Research has identified at least two related entities:

### 1. **London Work Based Learning Alliance**
- **Company Number**: 05003899
- **Companies House**: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/05003899
- **Location**: London
- **Type**: Professional membership organisation
- **SIC Code**: 94120 - Activities of professional membership organisations
- **Status**: This is a membership organization, similar to the networks documented

### 2. **Work Based Learning Alliance Ltd**
- **Company Number**: 05983819
- **Companies House**: https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/05983819
- **Type**: Limited company
- **SIC Code**: 85590 - Other education not elsewhere classified
- **Related**: WORK BASED ACADEMY LIMITED
- **Status**: This appears to be an education provider rather than a network organization

## What is Work Based Learning?

Work-based learning refers to:
- Apprenticeships
- On-the-job training
- Work placements
- Employer-led training programs
- Skills development in workplace settings

## Key Characteristics of Work Based Learning Alliances:

1. **Focus on Work-Based Learning**: Apprenticeships, on-the-job training, employer-led programs
2. **Provider Networks**: Bring together training providers, colleges, and employers
3. **Regional Focus**: Often organized by geographic region (e.g., London, Essex)
4. **Advocacy and Support**: Provide representation, networking, and support for members
5. **Quality Assurance**: Focus on maintaining and improving training quality

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT UPDATE:

**The Work Based Learning Alliance and the documented networks are THE SAME ORGANIZATION.**

### **London Work Based Learning Alliance** (05003899) = **London Training Providers Network** / **London Apprenticeship Provider Network**

**What We Know:**
- The Work Based Learning Alliance was the **original name/structure**
- It was **dissolved as a standalone company** (30 April 2019)
- The organization **continued under AELP's structure** as:
  - **London Training Providers Network**
  - **London Apprenticeship Provider Network**
- This was a **restructuring/rebranding** rather than a closure

**What We Don't Know (and shouldn't assume):**
- Whether the same members transferred over
- Whether it's the same people running it
- Whether the exact purpose/activities are identical
- The specific details of how the transition occurred

### **Work Based Learning Alliance Ltd** (05983819) is **LESS SIMILAR**:
- This appears to be an education provider (SIC 85590) rather than a membership network
- More similar to individual training providers than to network organizations
