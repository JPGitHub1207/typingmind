#### Content Batching Schedule

### 🎨 Content Type Templates

#### 1. Standard Feed Post
```
Image Requirements:
- Resolution: 1080x1350px (4:5 ratio)
- Character clearly visible
- High quality, no artifacts
- On-brand environment/styling

Caption Structure:
- Hook (first line visible in feed)
- Body (value/story)
- CTA
- Line break
- Hashtags (20-25)
```

#### 2. Carousel Post
```
Slide Structure:
1. Hook/Title slide
2-8. Value/Content slides
9. Summary/Recap
10. CTA slide

Design Consistency:
- Same color scheme throughout
- Character appears on 1, 5, 10 minimum
- Text readable on mobile
```

#### 3. Reels/TikTok
```
Video Structure:
- Hook: 0-3 seconds (pattern interrupt)
- Content: 4-45 seconds
- CTA: Last 3-5 seconds

Technical Specs:
- 1080x1920px (9:16)
- 15-60 seconds optimal
- Captions/subtitles required
- Trending audio when possible
```

#### 4. Story Content
```
Daily Stories Mix:
- 1-2 casual/lifestyle
- 1 engagement (poll/question)
- 1 behind-the-scenes
- 1 promotional (if applicable)
- 1 repost (tagged content/trends)
```

### 📸 Image Generation Workflow

#### Step-by-Step Process

```
1. CONCEPT
   ↓
   Define: pose, outfit, environment, mood, purpose
   ↓
2. PROMPT ENGINEERING  
   ↓
   Base prompt + character trigger + style modifiers
   ↓
3. GENERATION
   ↓
   Generate 4-8 variations
   ↓
4. SELECTION
   ↓
   Pick best 1-2 based on quality criteria
   ↓
5. POST-PROCESSING
   ↓
   - Face consistency check (FaceSwap if needed)
   - Color grading
   - Minor touch-ups
   - Resize for platform
   ↓
6. APPROVAL
   ↓
   Final quality check, add to approved folder
```

#### Quality Criteria Checklist
- [ ] Face matches reference (no drift)
- [ ] Hands look natural (or hidden)
- [ ] Proportions are correct
- [ ] No artifacts or distortions
- [ ] Lighting is consistent
- [ ] Outfit matches character style
- [ ] Environment is appropriate
- [ ] Image tells a story

### 📈 Growth Tactics

#### Organic Growth Strategies

**1. Hashtag Strategy**
```
Per Post (25-30 total):
- 5 large (1M+ posts): broad reach
- 10 medium (100K-1M): targeted reach  
- 10 small (10K-100K): community building
- 5 niche-specific: authority building

Rotate sets to avoid shadowban
Track performance weekly
```

**2. Engagement Pods (Ethical)**
- Join 2-3 pods in your niche
- Engage authentically (not just likes)
- Time-box to 30 min/day

**3. Collaboration Strategy**
```
Month 4: Micro-influencers (1K-10K)
- Offer: Free content/value
- Goal: 5 collaborations
- Expected gain: 200-500 followers each

Month 5: Small influencers (10K-50K)
- Offer: Cross-promotion + value
- Goal: 3 collaborations
- Expected gain: 500-2K followers each

Month 6: Medium influencers (50K-100K)
- Offer: Paid collaboration or significant value
- Goal: 1-2 collaborations
- Expected gain: 2K-5K followers each
```

**4. Content Virality Tactics**
- Ride trends within 24-48 hours
- Create "save-worthy" content (tutorials, tips)
- Polarizing opinions (careful, on-brand)
- Relatable struggles/wins
- Before/after transformations

**5. Cross-Platform Funnel**
```
TikTok (discovery) 
    ↓
Instagram (community)
    ↓
YouTube (deep engagement)
    ↓
Newsletter/Discord (owned audience)
```

#### Paid Growth (Optional)

**Instagram/Facebook Ads:**
- Budget: $10-20/day to start
- Objective: Profile visits or engagement
- Target: Interests aligned with niche
- Creative: Best performing organic content

**Influencer Shoutouts:**
- $50-200 for story mentions
- $100-500 for feed posts
- ROI tracking essential

## Risk Mitigation

### ⚠️ Risk Matrix

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Platform policy change | Medium | High | Diversify platforms, build email list |
| Audience backlash | Low | Medium | Be transparent about AI nature |
| Tech failure | Low | Medium | Backup systems, documented processes |
| Visual inconsistency | Medium | High | Invest in good LoRA, QA process |
| Burnout | Medium | Medium | Batch content, automate, delegate |
| Competition | High | Low | Focus on unique positioning |
| Algorithm changes | High | Medium | Diversify content types, platforms |

### 🛡️ Contingency Plans

**If growth stalls:**
1. Audit content performance
2. Test new content formats
3. Increase collaboration efforts
4. Consider paid promotion
5. Pivot niche slightly if needed

**If platform bans AI content:**
1. Migrate to alternative platforms
2. Focus on owned platforms (website, newsletter)
3. Pivot to "AI-assisted" positioning
4. Explore B2B opportunities

**If monetization lags:**
1. Lower pricing to get testimonials
2. Focus on affiliate income
3. Create digital products
4. Offer services (consulting, content creation)
