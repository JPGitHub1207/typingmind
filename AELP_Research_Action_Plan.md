- **Email:** info@aelp.org.uk or events@aelp.org.uk
- **Phone:** Check website for events team number
- **Contact Form:** https://www.aelp.org.uk/contact

**What to Ask For:**
> "Hi, I'm registered for the 2025 National Conference (June 23, 2025). Could you please provide:
> 1. The full conference program including speaker/facilitator names for each workshop session

> 4. List of regular workshop providers/consultants who work with AELP"

## Alternative Approaches

### If AELP Doesn't Respond:
1. **Ask fellow attendees** - Connect with others going to conference
2. **Check exhibitor list** - Workshop providers often exhibit
3. **Review AELP's partner/sponsor pages** - Regular collaborators listed
4. **Freedom of Information request** - If government funding involved
5. **Contact specific organizations** - Ask if they've worked with AELP

### If Historical Info is Limited:
1. **Focus on 2024-2025 only** - Most recent data
2. **Interview AELP members** - They may remember past speakers
3. **Check video archives** - AELP may have recorded sessions
4. **Review member directories** - Active providers often members
