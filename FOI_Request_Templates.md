# FOI Request Templates - Ready to Use

## Quick Start: Submit These FOI Requests Today

### Request 1: DfE - Levy Paying Employers

**To:** foi.requests@education.gov.uk
**Subject:** Freedom of Information Request - Apprenticeship Levy Data

**Template:**

> Dear Freedom of Information Team,
> 
> I am writing to request the following information under the Freedom of Information Act 2000:
> 
> 1. A list of all employers in England who paid the apprenticeship levy in the financial year 2024/25 (or most recent available data), including:
>    - Company/organisation name
>    - Approximate number of employees (if available)
>    - Industry sector (if available)
> 
> 2. For each employer, the number of apprenticeship starts in the last 12 months (or most recent available data).
> 
> 3. The total amount of levy funds used by each employer in the last 12 months (or most recent available data).
> 
> If this information is not available in the exact format requested, please provide what is available. If some information is exempt from disclosure, please provide the information that can be disclosed.
> 
> I understand that you have 20 working days to respond to this request.
> 
> Thank you for your assistance.
> 
> Yours sincerely,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Contact Details]

### Request 3: DfE - Large Employers with Apprenticeships

**To:** foi.requests@education.gov.uk
**Subject:** Freedom of Information Request - Large Employers Apprenticeship Data

**Template:**

> Dear Freedom of Information Team,
> 
> I am writing to request the following information under the Freedom of Information Act 2000:
> 
> A list of all employers in England with:
> - 1,000 or more employees, AND
> - 10 or more apprenticeship starts in the last 12 months (or most recent available data)
> 
> For each employer, please include:
> - Company/organisation name
> - Number of employees (if available)
> - Number of apprenticeship starts in last 12 months
> - Industry sector (if available)
> 
> If this information is not available in the exact format requested, please provide what is available.
> 
> I understand that you have 20 working days to respond to this request.
> 
> Thank you for your assistance.
> 
> Yours sincerely,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Contact Details]

|--------------|----------|-----------|-------------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|------------------------|--------|----------|-------|

**Fill as you research:**
- Company Name: From your research
- Industry: If known
- Employees: If known (from LinkedIn/website)
- Levy Payer?: Yes/No/Unknown (will know after FOI)
- Has Apprenticeships?: Yes/No (from website research)
- Apprenticeship Manager: LinkedIn profile link
- L&D Manager: LinkedIn profile link
- Source: FOI/LinkedIn/FTSE/Awards/etc.
