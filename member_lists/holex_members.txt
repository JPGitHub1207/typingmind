1. HOLEX’s Budget 2025 submission – A New Path for Growth: The People’s Skills Solution– sets out a clear vision for growth built around people, their potential, and lifelong learning.
2. In its submission, HOLEX calls for:
3. 💷Fair and increased funding: a £5 billion boost to the adult skills budget over this Parliament, targeted through local networks that understand community and employer needs;
4. 🎓 Equity of educational experience: consistent, high-quality provision for all adults, including those with SEND and those furthest from work;
5. 📈Recognition of adult education: as a key driver of economic growth, wellbeing, and social cohesion; and
6. 🤝Stronger cross-government coordination: bringing together DfE, DWP, MHCLG, MCAs, and local authorities to make lifelong learning more responsive and accountable.
