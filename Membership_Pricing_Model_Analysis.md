# Membership Pricing Model Analysis - Critical Evaluation

## Your Proposed Model

### Tier 1: Entry - £100/month (£1,200/year)
- 1 full day event each quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 20% off training (2 half days per quarter = 8 per year)

### Tier 2: Member - £280/month (£3,360/year)
- 2 places (2 people can attend)
- Rising star network (shadow, supported)
- All training included (free)
- 1 full day event (per quarter? unclear)
- Monthly meetups
- 2 online half days each month (24 per year)

### Tier 3: Growth - £420/month (£5,040/year)
- 3 places (3 people can attend)
- All above benefits
- All training free
- 6 online half days (unlimited? unclear)

**Upsell:** Training courses (additional revenue)

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES

### 1. **PRICING IS TOO LOW - You're Undervaluing**

**The Problem:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**Reality Check:**
- You're targeting large employers (corporate budgets)
- You're providing significant value (events, training, support)
- Your costs are high (venues, catering, speakers, your time)
- **You're pricing like a small business, not a premium network**

**Comparison:**
- CIPD: £120-200/year (but that's for individuals, not networks)
- Professional networks: £2,000-10,000/year
- Corporate training: £500-2,000 per day
- **You're way too cheap**

**The Math:**
- Entry: £1,200/year
- If you have 50 Entry members = £60,000/year
- But: 4 events × 50 people = 200 event attendances
- Cost per event: £5,000-10,000
- 4 events = £20,000-40,000
- Plus your time, platform, etc.
- **You're losing money or barely breaking even**

### 2. **VALUE PROPOSITION IS CONFUSED**

**The Problem:**
- Entry gets "20% off training" - but training costs what?
- Member gets "all training free" - but what training?
- Growth gets "6 online half days (unlimited?)" - unclear

**Questions:**
- What's the regular price of training? (to calculate 20% off)
- What training is included vs. upsell?
- How many training sessions are there?
- What's the value of "free training"?

**Reality:**
- If training is valuable, why give it away?
- If training isn't valuable, why include it?
- **Unclear value = hard to sell**

### 3. **"RISING STAR NETWORK" - What Is This?**

**The Problem:**
- "Rising star network (shadow, supported)"
- What does this mean?
- What's the value?
- How do you deliver it?

**Questions:**
- Is this mentoring?
- Is this a separate network?
- What's the time commitment?
- How do you scale this?

**Reality:**
- Sounds valuable but undefined
- Hard to explain to customers
- Hard to deliver at scale
- **Needs clarification**

### 4. **"PLACES" - Unclear Value**

**The Problem:**
- Member: "2 places" - 2 people can attend?
- Growth: "3 places" - 3 people can attend?
- But Entry: 1 place? (not stated)

**Questions:**
- Is this 2 people per event?
- 2 people total for the year?
- Can they be different people?
- What's the value of multiple places?

**Reality:**
- For large employers, multiple places = value
- But needs to be clear
- **Unclear = confusion = objections**

### 5. **UPSELL MODEL - Risky**

**The Problem:**
- "Upsell training courses"
- But Member and Growth get "all training free"
- So only Entry pays for training?

**Questions:**
- What training is upsell vs. included?
- How do you upsell if it's "free"?
- What's the revenue from upsells?
- Does this create confusion?

**Reality:**
- Upsell model can work
- But needs clear separation
- **Confusing model = lower conversion**

### 6. **COST STRUCTURE - Does It Work?**

**The Math:**

**Entry Tier (£100/month = £1,200/year):**
- 4 full day events
- 12 monthly meetups
- Cost per event: £5,000-10,000
- 4 events = £20,000-40,000
- 12 meetups = £12,000-24,000 (if £1,000-2,000 each)
- **Total costs: £32,000-64,000 for 50 members**
- **Revenue: £60,000**
- **Margin: -£4,000 to +£28,000 (risky)**

**Member Tier (£280/month = £3,360/year):**
- 2 places = 2× the event costs
- All training free = no training revenue
- Higher costs, same price pressure
- **Even riskier**

**Growth Tier (£420/month = £5,040/year):**
- 3 places = 3× the event costs
- All training free = no training revenue
- **Most risky**

**Reality:**
- Your costs scale with places
- But revenue doesn't scale proportionally
- **Math doesn't work**

## 💡 RECOMMENDED FIXES

### Fix 1: Increase Prices Significantly

**Current:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**Recommended:**
- Entry: £200/month = £2,400/year (2× increase)
- Member: £500/month = £6,000/year (1.8× increase)
- Growth: £750/month = £9,000/year (1.5× increase)

**Why:**
- Corporate budgets can handle this
- Better reflects value
- Better unit economics
- More sustainable

### Fix 2: Clarify Value Proposition

**Entry - £2,400/year:**
- 4 full day events per year
- 12 monthly meetups
- Access to online resources
- 20% discount on premium training courses

**Member - £6,000/year:**
- Everything in Entry, PLUS:
- 2 places (2 people can attend events)
- Rising star network access (defined program)
- 2 free premium training courses per quarter (8 per year)
- Priority booking

**Growth - £9,000/year:**
- Everything in Member, PLUS:
- 3 places (3 people can attend events)
- Unlimited premium training courses
- 1:1 quarterly strategy session
- Exclusive Growth-only events

### Fix 3: Define "Rising Star Network"

**What It Is:**
- Mentoring program for junior L&D professionals
- Quarterly group sessions
- Access to senior members
- Career development support

**How to Deliver:**
- Quarterly group mentoring sessions
- Online community
- Resource library
- Can scale with group format

### Fix 4: Clarify Training Model

**Included Training (Free for Member/Growth):**
- 2 half-day sessions per quarter (standard)
- Online resources
- Recordings

**Premium Training (Upsell):**
- Advanced courses
- Specialist topics
- Extended programs
- Entry gets 20% off, Member/Growth get some free, can buy more

### Fix 5: Fix the Math

**New Pricing:**
- Entry: £2,400/year
- Member: £6,000/year
- Growth: £9,000/year

**Break-Even:**
- Entry: Need 15-20 members to break even
- Member: Need 8-10 members to break even
- Growth: Need 5-7 members to break even

**More Realistic:**
- Can actually make money
- Sustainable model
- Can scale
