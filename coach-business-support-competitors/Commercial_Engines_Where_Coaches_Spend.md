# Where independent coaches spend meaningful money

**Research date:** 3 September 2026  
**Question:** Where is the strongest public evidence that coaches (and people becoming coaches) spend real money — and what that means for a Four Corners Coaching-style business-support offer.

**Method:** Public websites, published prices, founder claims, Trustpilot/volume signals, and already-retrieved Companies House balance sheets. Turnover is **not** filed for these small companies (s.444 / micro). Balance-sheet size is a weak proxy only.

**Correction:** Better Bolder Braver’s latest iXBRL shows `NetAssetsLiabilities` with `sign="-"` → **−£619** (was wrongly reported as +£619). Same sign check also flips Fearless Business to **−£48,398** and Business Coach Academy to **−£10,477**.

Labels used below: **FACT** / **INFERENCE** / **UNKNOWN**.

## Focus businesses

### 1. Barefoot Coaching (06932330) — and Holdings (15307680)

#### What they sell (FACT)

1. **Flagship ICF Level 2 coach training** — £7,395 (personal incl. VAT; company +VAT). Optional University of Chester PG Cert add-on **£2,280**.  
2. **Post-qualification craft CPD** — Supervision £4,450 · Team Coaching £3,650 · Grief & Loss £3,500 · Group Coaching short £810 · conference £207 · supervision sessions £108.  
3. **Up & Running** — year-long **business** programme for already-trained coaches — **£999** (or £699 if bought with flagship).  
4. **Leader as Coach** — managers/leaders, not career coaches — **£2,500** pp (open; also sold in-house).  
5. Corporate / organisational coaching culture and in-house leadership programmes (Amazon, Channel 4, Lloyds named in Barefoot news).  
6. Low-ticket tools/cards (£15–£50).

#### Who pays (FACT / INFERENCE)

- Individuals becoming coaches (self-funded and company-funded seats on Level 2).  
- Practising coaches buying CPD / supervision.  
- **Employers** buying Leader as Coach and in-house programmes.  
- Up & Running buyers are trained coaches — but at £999 this is not the cash engine.

#### Revenue drivers ranked by commercial weight (INFERENCE)

| Driver | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Coach **qualification / accredited training** | **Primary** | £7k+ ticket · multi-month · alumni claims in thousands · 25+ year brand |
| Corporate / leader-as-coach | **Major** | Named enterprise clients · separate product line · company-funded pricing |
| Craft CPD / supervision / specialist certs | **Secondary cash** | £2k–£4.5k tickets · credential renewal (40 CCEs) |
| Business-of-coaching (Up & Running) | **Peripheral** | Only £999 · clearly add-on to training funnel |
| Events / merch | Peripheral | Conference £207 · cards |

#### Core commercial engine

**Becoming a qualified coach + staying credentialed + selling coaching skills into organisations.**  
Not “help coaches get clients” as the main business.

#### Volume / scale evidence (FACT unless noted)

- Claimed **4,000+ alumni** (Coach’s Journey podcast interview with Kim Morgan).  
- Trustpilot presence (100+ reviews class).  
- Trading co: **9 employees**, cash **£513,977**, current assets **£1,219,615**, creditors **£1,243,909**, net assets only **£36,907** (year to 31 Dec 2025).  
- **INFERENCE:** Large creditors vs cash is consistent with a training business holding **deferred course income / payables**, not a lean solo mentor shop.  
- Holdings: SIC **64209 holding company**; **Investments in subsidiaries £4,371,750**; net assets **£1,521,732**; cash only £18k; employees 2. Incorporated Nov 2023; charge registered Dec 2023; PSC shift so Holdings owns Barefoot Coaching Ltd.  
- **INFERENCE:** Holdings’ £1.52m equity and £4.37m subsidiary investment reflect a **2023 group restructuring / ownership revaluation** of the long-running training business — not a separate “business support for coaches” product line. Value was created by **qualification + corporate training over ~15–25 years**, then capitalised in the holding co.

#### Helps coaches…?

| Need | Barefoot? |
|---|---|
| Become qualified | **Yes — core** |
| Become better coaches | **Yes — CPD / supervision** |
| Grow their business | Weakly (Up & Running £999) |
| Find clients | Not the main offer |
| Access paid coaching work | Indirect (credential + brand); not a placement model |

#### Vs TCR / BBB

Barefoot is a **school + corporate L&D supplier**. TCR/BBB sell **marketing/business support to already-interested coaches**. Barefoot’s balance-sheet scale is an order of magnitude above both — and its cheap business add-on shows it does **not** believe post-qualification business support is where the money is.

## Barefoot: what created the scale?

**FACT chain:**

1. Trading company founded 2009; Kim Morgan’s coach-training brand older still (~2001 flagship narrative).  
2. Product mix dominated by **£7k+ accredited training**, stacked **£3–4.5k CPD**, and **employer-funded** leader/coach culture work.  
3. Up & Running business support is **£999** — an order of magnitude cheaper than Joanna Lott / TCR / Fearless — signalling secondary status.  
4. Dec 2023: Barefoot Coaching Holdings Ltd becomes parent; £4.37m investment in subsidiary on Holdings balance sheet; charge registered; Andy Chandler as CEO alongside Kim Morgan.  
5. Trading co shows high cash **and** high creditors with thin equity — typical of a busy training operation; economic value of the group sits in Holdings’ investment asset / equity (£1.52m), not in “business mentoring for coaches.”

**INFERENCE:** Scale = **qualification school + corporate L&D**, compounded over decades, then **holding-company capitalisation**. It is **not** evidence that post-qualification business-support memberships create Barefoot-like balance sheets.

## Answers to the objective questions

### 1. Where does the strongest evidence suggest coaches spend the most money?

**Qualification and credentialled craft training — led by Barefoot-class schools — then high buy-ins to “become a business coach” (franchise / academy).**  

Evidence strength order:

1. **Barefoot-scale accredited training + corporate** — employees, cash, creditors, alumni claims, £7k tickets, group structure.  
2. **Franchise / academy entry tickets** (£9.9k–£22.5k) — published prices; CH only modest; claims noisy.  
3. **Mid-ticket business-of-coaching** (£3k–£6k) — Joanna Lott / TCR; Joanna’s rising net assets are the cleanest small-company signal in that niche.  
4. **Low-ticket membership** (BBB £50–60/mo) — visible, not balance-sheet strong.

### 2. Is business support for coaches commercially attractive in its own right?

**Yes at boutique scale; not proven at Barefoot scale.**  

Joanna Lott (**£48k net assets**, 200+ coaches claim, £3.3k+VAT core SKU) and TCR (durable product ladder) show a real willingness to pay. BBB and Fearless/BCA filings show that **visibility and high prices do not guarantee a capitalised company**. Attractive as a **founder-led £low-hundreds-k** practice; weak evidence it is a category that builds £1m+ group equity without attaching to qualification or corporate.

### 3. Which business-support products appear strongest?

1. **Joanna Lott — Business of Coaching** (£3,300+VAT, ladder, ICF CCE, improving CH).  
2. **TCR Momentum ladder** (higher tickets, clear private vs corporate client split).  
3. **Packaged client-getting + certification for career changers** (BCA / UK Growth Coach) — strong *price* evidence, weaker *quality of CH/outcome* evidence.  

Barefoot’s Up & Running is **not** strong as a standalone engine.

### 4. Does qualification/training appear much stronger than post-qualification business support?

**Yes.** Barefoot’s economics dwarf the business-support peers. Employers and aspiring coaches pay multi-thousand pounds for **credentials and craft**; post-qualification business help clears lower absolute spend and much smaller firms.

### 5. Is helping coaches access clients/work a stronger proposition?

**As a sales message, yes — almost every surviving business-support brand leads with clients/income.**  
**As a balance-sheet outcome, mixed:** Joanna/TCR suggest it sells; Fearless/BCA show risk; **nobody here runs a verified placement marketplace**. Franchise/academy models sell “access to clients” as part of a **career-entry package**, which is a different (and higher-ticket) buy than marketing membership for already-qualified coaches.

### 6. Which 3 businesses should Four Corners Coaching study most closely — and why?

1. **Barefoot Coaching (group)** — Not to copy Up & Running. To understand **where the real money sits** (qualification + employer L&D), how deferred income and alumni compounds, and why business support is priced as an add-on. Prevents false analogies from their cash pile.  
2. **Joanna Lott Coaching** — Best current template for **honest, mid-ticket, post-qualification business building** aimed at professional coaches: ladder, pricing, ICF packaging, and the only peer with a clearly strengthening solo balance sheet.  
3. **The Coaching Revolution** — Closest pure competitor to “help coaches get clients”: product architecture (private vs corporate buyers), pricing power, and commercial hygiene — even though CH remains micro.

**Honourable mention:** UK Growth Coach / Business Coach Academy if Four Corners ever considers **career-entry / packaged practice** rather than support for already-qualified coaches — different customer, higher ticket, different ethics/risk profile.
