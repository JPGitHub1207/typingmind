# 30-Day Action Plan: European Space Tech Event

**Critical Mentor's Note**: This is a sprint plan. Execute rigorously. If you can't complete these steps in 30 days, you may not have the execution capability to run an event.

---

## Week 1: Foundation & Research

### Day 1-2: Define Your Niche

**Task**: Answer these questions in writing (be specific):

1. **What specific problem are you solving?**
   - Example: "Space tech startups struggle to connect with enterprise buyers"
   - Example: "European space tech investors lack a dedicated forum to see deal flow"
   - ❌ Bad: "People need space tech events"

2. **Who is your target audience?** (Be specific)
   - Example: "Series A/B space tech startups (€5M-€50M raised) seeking enterprise customers"
   - Example: "VCs and corporate VCs investing €500K-€5M in European space tech"
   - ❌ Bad: "Space tech companies"

3. **What's your unique value proposition?**
   - What makes you different from existing events?
   - Why will people choose you over alternatives?

4. **What's your event concept?**
   - Format: (Summit, Workshop, Matchmaking, etc.)
   - Duration: (1 day, 2 days, etc.)
   - Size: (100, 200, 500, etc.)
   - Location: (City, Virtual, Hybrid)

**Deliverable**: 1-page concept document

---

### Day 3-4: Competitive Research

**Task**: Research top 5 competitors using the Competitor Research Template

**Focus on:**
- Pricing (tickets, sponsorships)
- Format and content
- Target audience
- What they do well
- What they do poorly
- Gaps in the market

**Deliverable**: Completed competitor analysis (see COMPETITOR_RESEARCH_TEMPLATE.md)

---

### Day 5-7: Initial Validation

**Task**: Create and distribute validation survey

**Steps:**
1. Create survey (use Google Forms, Typeform, etc.)
   - Use questions from VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK.md
   - Keep it short (5-7 questions max)

2. Distribute to 50-100 people:
   - LinkedIn: Post in space tech groups
   - Direct outreach: Message 20-30 people directly
   - Email: If you have a list
   - Forums: Reddit r/space, industry forums

3. Conduct 3-5 interviews:
   - Reach out to potential attendees
   - Ask deeper questions (see VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK.md)
   - Take notes

**Deliverable**: 
- Survey results (aim for 30+ responses)
- Interview notes
- Key insights document

---

## Week 2: Refinement & Landing Page

### Day 8-10: Refine Concept Based on Feedback

**Task**: Update your concept based on validation learnings

**Questions to answer:**
- Did validation confirm your assumptions?
- What needs to change?
- What resonated most with people?
- What concerns did they raise?

**Deliverable**: Revised concept document

---

### Day 11-14: Create Landing Page & Pre-Sell

**Task**: Build a simple landing page to test interest

**Tools** (choose one):
- Carrd.co (simplest, €9/month)
- Webflow (more control, free tier)
- Framer (modern, free tier)
- Even: Simple HTML page

**Must include:**
- Event name and tagline
- Value proposition (what problem you solve)
- Target audience
- Key topics (even if TBD)
- Tentative date/location
- Pricing tiers
- "Reserve Your Spot" or "Get Early Access" button

**Marketing:**
- Share on LinkedIn (personal + groups)
- Email to your network
- Post in relevant forums
- Run small LinkedIn/Google ads (€50-€100 budget)

**Goal**: 25-50 signups in 2 weeks

**Deliverable**: Landing page live, tracking signups

---

## Week 3: Pre-Sales & Sponsors

### Day 15-18: Pre-Sell Tickets

**Task**: Try to pre-sell 20-30 tickets

**Offer:**
- "Founding Member" early bird pricing (30-40% off)
- Money-back guarantee
- Exclusive benefits (VIP access, etc.)

**Outreach:**
- Contact survey/interview respondents who showed interest
- Reach out to your network
- Use landing page signups

**Goal**: 20-30 paid commitments

**Deliverable**: Pre-sales tracker (spreadsheet)

---

### Day 19-21: Sponsor Outreach

**Task**: Create sponsor prospectus and reach out to 20-30 potential sponsors

**Sponsor Prospectus should include:**
- Event concept and value proposition
- Target audience demographics
- Expected attendance
- Sponsorship packages (Platinum €50K, Gold €25K, Silver €10K)
- What's included
- Why this event is valuable

**Outreach:**
- Warm introductions (use your network)
- Cold emails (personalized)
- LinkedIn messages

**Goal**: 2-3 anchor sponsors committed (€10K-€25K each)

**Deliverable**: Sponsor prospectus, outreach tracker

---

## Week 4: Advisory Board & Go/No-Go

### Day 22-25: Recruit Advisory Board

**Task**: Recruit 3-5 industry veterans

**Who to target:**
- Former executives (Airbus, OHB, etc.)
- Well-known investors
- Industry thought leaders
- Former ESA/government officials

**What to offer:**
- Free tickets
- Speaking opportunity (optional)
- Recognition
- Input on event direction

**Outreach:**
- Use your network for introductions
- LinkedIn messages
- Email

**Goal**: 3-5 advisors committed, at least 2 are "name brand"

**Deliverable**: Advisory board list, commitments

---

**Task**: Evaluate all validation results and make decision

**Use Decision Matrix** (from VALIDATION_FRAMEWORK.md):

|----------|--------|--------------|----------|

## Critical Success Factors

### Must-Haves to Proceed:

1. ✅ **Clear niche** - Not generic
2. ✅ **Validated demand** - Survey + interviews show interest
3. ✅ **Pre-sales** - 20+ paid commitments
4. ✅ **Sponsor interest** - At least 1 committed
5. ✅ **Advisory support** - 2+ advisors committed
6. ✅ **Personal readiness** - Time, resources, commitment

### Red Flags (Don't Proceed If):

1. ❌ Can't define clear niche
2. ❌ Validation shows weak interest (<30% positive)
3. ❌ Can't pre-sell tickets
4. ❌ No sponsor interest
5. ❌ Can't get advisors
6. ❌ Don't have resources/time

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Analysis Paralysis**
   - Don't research forever. Set deadlines and stick to them.

2. **Perfectionism**
   - Landing page doesn't need to be perfect. Good enough to test is fine.

3. **Fear of Rejection**
   - You'll get "no"s. That's data. Learn from them.

4. **Talking, Not Doing**
   - Execution > planning. Do the work.

5. **Ignoring Red Flags**
   - If validation fails, don't force it. Pivot.
