## 🚨 RED FLAGS CHECKLIST

**STOP if any of these are true:**

- [ ] ❌ Can't articulate value prop in one sentence
- [ ] ❌ <20 people willing to pay at validation
- [ ] ❌ No clear differentiation from competitors
- [ ] ❌ Budget shows >€50k loss without funding secured
- [ ] ❌ Can't commit 20+ hours/week for 12 months
- [ ] ❌ No event production experience and can't hire it
- [ ] ❌ Major objections raised with no mitigation plan
- [ ] ❌ Doing this primarily for personal brand

**If 3+ red flags: STOP or MAJOR PIVOT REQUIRED**

---

## ✅ GREEN LIGHTS CHECKLIST

**PROCEED if all of these are true:**

- [ ] ✅ Clear one-sentence value proposition
- [ ] ✅ 30+ validated prospects (scored 4+ on WTP)
- [ ] ✅ Structural differentiation (not just "better")
- [ ] ✅ Financial model shows break-even or profit path
- [ ] ✅ €200k+ budget secured or committed
- [ ] ✅ Team has skills or budget to hire them
- [ ] ✅ 5+ advisory board members committed
- [ ] ✅ 12+ months available before launch
- [ ] ✅ Clear mitigation for top 3 objections
- [ ] ✅ Solving real market problem (not personal goal)

**If 8+ green lights: STRONG GO**
