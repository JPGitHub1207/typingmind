## Key Insights from Research

### Most Likely Presenters:

1. **Funding Zone** → DfE/Apprenticeship Service (government)
2. **Policy Zone** → Ofsted inspectors (government/consultants)
3. **Employer Zone** → NHS representatives (government/healthcare)
4. **Technology Zone** → Digital badging platform providers (commercial)
5. **Innovation Zone** → Data analytics/EdTech companies (commercial)
6. **Gen Z Zone** → Careers education organizations (NGO/commercial)
7. **Growth Zone** → Business engagement consultants (commercial)
8. **Partnership Zone** → Policy consultants/sector bodies (mixed)
9. **Teaching & Learning Zone** → CPD providers (commercial/NGO)

### Important Notes:

- **Government workshops** (Funding, Policy) are likely delivered by official representatives
- **Technology workshops** often feature sponsor/exhibitor presentations
- **Some workshops** may be delivered by AELP staff or members
- **Presenter information** is typically released closer to conference date
- **Previous conferences** may follow similar patterns
