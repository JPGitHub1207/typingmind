# Market Prioritization Framework - Quick Decision Guide

## The Two Markets at a Glance

### Market 1: Employer Providers (100 companies)
- **Who:** Fully fledged training providers (large employers who became providers)
- **Pain:** Compliance, Ofsted, quality management (operational)
- **Price:** £3,000-10,000/year
- **Big Names:** Possible but limited

### Market 2: Large Levy Employers (100,000+ companies)
- **Who:** Large companies with L&D/Apprenticeship Managers
- **Pain:** Growth and Skills Levy decisions, provider management (strategic)
- **Price:** £1,500-5,000/year
- **Big Names:** Excellent (FTSE 100, household brands)

---

## 🎯 DECISION FRAMEWORK

### If Your Goal is "Big Names" → Start with Market 2

**Why:**
- FTSE 100 = household brands
- Higher credibility
- Attracts others
- "If X is in it, it must be good"

**Strategy:**
1. Target 10-15 FTSE 100 companies first
2. Get them as advisory board or early members
3. Use for credibility: "Join [Big Company 1], [Big Company 2]..."
4. Then expand to broader market

**Timeline:** Month 1-2 get big names, Month 3-4 launch network

---

### If Your Goal is "Quick Revenue" → Start with Market 1

**Why:**
- Smaller, focused market (easier to reach)
- Higher conversion rate (10-30%)
- Higher price point (£5,000/year)
- More urgent need (compliance)

**Strategy:**
1. Target 100 employer providers
2. Position as "operational support network"
3. Get "outstanding providers" as speakers
4. Launch with 10-20 members quickly

**Timeline:** Month 1-2 validate, Month 3-4 launch, Month 5-6 scale

---

### If Your Goal is "Maximum Revenue" → Dual Track (Recommended)

**Why:**
- Two revenue streams
- Balanced approach
- Scalable
- Synergies between groups

**Strategy:**
1. **Month 1-2:** Get big names (Market 2) for credibility
2. **Month 3-4:** Launch Employer Provider Network (Market 1)
3. **Month 5-6:** Launch Large Employer Network (Market 2)
4. **Month 7+:** Create synergies, scale both

**Revenue:**
- Year 1: £225,000 (20 providers + 50 large employers)
- Year 2: £525,000 (30 providers + 150 large employers)

## 🎯 RECOMMENDED APPROACH: "Big Names First, Then Dual Track"

### Phase 1: Get Big Names (Month 1-2)

**Target:** 10-15 FTSE 100 companies
**Focus:** L&D/Apprenticeship Managers
**Approach:** Advisory board or early membership
**Goal:** Credibility and social proof

**Why First:**
- Gets you "big names" quickly
- Creates credibility
- Attracts others
- Makes both networks more attractive

### Phase 2: Launch Employer Provider Network (Month 3-4)

**Target:** 100 employer providers
**Price:** £5,000/year
**Focus:** Operational support (compliance, quality)
**Goal:** 10-20 members, prove model

**Why Second:**
- Easier to reach (smaller market)
- Higher conversion
- Higher price point
- Quick revenue

### Phase 3: Launch Large Employer Network (Month 5-6)

**Target:** Large levy employers
**Price:** £2,500/year
**Focus:** Preemptive/strategic (Growth and Skills Levy decisions)
**Goal:** 20-30 members, scale

**Why Third:**
- Use big names for credibility
- "Join [Big Company 1], [Big Company 2]..."
- Attracts others
- Scales to 100+ members

### Phase 4: Create Synergies (Month 7+)

**Approach:** Cross-network events
**Value:** Both groups learn from each other
**Goal:** Scale both, create platform

**Why:**
- Higher value
- More attractive
- Can charge premium
- Sustainable growth

## 💰 REVENUE SCENARIOS

### Scenario 1: Employer Providers Only
- 20 members × £5,000 = **£100,000/year**
- Pros: Quick revenue, easier
- Cons: Limited scalability

### Scenario 2: Large Employers Only
- 100 members × £2,500 = **£250,000/year**
- Pros: Scalable, big names
- Cons: Harder to reach

### Scenario 3: Dual Track (Recommended)
- 20 providers × £5,000 = £100,000
- 50 large employers × £2,500 = £125,000
- **Total: £225,000/year**
- Year 2: **£525,000/year** (30 + 150)
- Pros: Balanced, scalable, big names
- Cons: More complex

## 🎯 "BIG NAMES" STRATEGY

### How to Get Big Names:

1. **Target FTSE 100 First:**
   - LinkedIn to L&D/Apprenticeship Managers
   - Personal connections
   - Industry contacts

2. **Advisory Board Approach:**
   - "Help shape this network"
   - Early access
   - Thought leadership
   - Discount/free

3. **Value Proposition:**
   - "Exclusive network for large employers"
   - "Learn from other FTSE 100 companies"
   - "Strategic guidance on Growth and Skills Levy"

4. **Credibility Building:**
   - "Join [Big Company 1], [Big Company 2]..."
   - Social proof
   - Attracts others

5. **Free Pilot Event:**
   - "Understanding Growth and Skills Levy"
   - Invite big names
   - Educational, not salesy
   - Gauge interest
