## 🎨 Content Strategy Highlights

### Content Types (Priority Order)
1. **Quick Facts** (15-30 seconds) - High engagement, easy to produce
2. **Historical Figures** (45-60 seconds) - Personal connection, shareable
3. **Historical Events** (60 seconds) - Educational value, depth
4. **Myth Debunked** (30-45 seconds) - Viral potential, engaging
5. **On This Day** (30 seconds) - Timely, consistent content
6. **Historical Mysteries** (60 seconds) - Intrigue, longer engagement

### High-Engagement Topics
- Ancient civilizations (Egypt, Rome, Greece)
- World Wars (WWI, WWII)
- Historical mysteries and conspiracies
- Famous historical figures
- Medieval history
- Lesser-known historical facts

### Posting Strategy
- **Frequency**: Daily (1-3 posts per day)
- **Platforms**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts (same content, optimized)
- **Timing**: Peak hours (varies by platform and audience)
- **Consistency**: Regular schedule, consistent style
