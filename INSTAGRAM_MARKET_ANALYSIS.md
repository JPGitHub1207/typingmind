### 1. Sponsored Posts (Most Common)
- **Frequency**: 1-3 per week for large accounts

### 2. Affiliate Marketing
- **Common**: Book promotions, course platforms

### 3. Brand Partnerships
- **Types**: Museum partnerships, educational institutions, media companies
- **Value**: Varies widely, can be significant for large accounts
- **Frequency**: Ongoing partnerships

### 4. Merchandise Sales
- **Types**: T-shirts, posters, books, educational materials

- **Frequency**: Regular promotions

### 5. Premium Content
- **Types**: Extended content, courses, exclusive posts
