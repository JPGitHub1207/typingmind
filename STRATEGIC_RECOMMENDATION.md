## 🎯 OPPORTUNITY #1: Preemptive Large Levy Employer Network

### Why This is Your Best Bet

**1. Policy Timing = Urgency**
- Government policy change creates immediate need
- Employers need to understand opportunity NOW
- Early mover advantage (get in before competition)
- Time-sensitive = premium pricing justified

**2. High Value Proposition**
- **Preventive value** - Help them avoid mistakes (like Citron)
- **Decision support** - Help them decide IF they should become providers
- **Implementation guidance** - Help them do it RIGHT if they do
- **Risk mitigation** - Avoid costly failures

**3. Corporate Budgets = Premium Pricing**
- Large employers have corporate budgets
- Not training provider margins
- Can charge £3,000-10,000+ per year
- They're used to paying for expertise

**4. Large Market**
- 100+ potential members
- Growing (policy-driven)
- Less competition (new opportunity)

**5. Unique Position**
- "Preemptive" = different from reactive support
- Help BEFORE they commit
- Help them make informed decisions
- Less competition in this space

### The Opportunity

**Government Policy Change:**
- Large employers can now use apprenticeship levy for units (short courses)
- Much more appealing than giving £100k to providers
- Creates opportunity for employers to become providers
- But they need guidance to do it right

**Your Value:**
- Help them understand the opportunity
- Guide them through requirements
- Help them decide if it's right for them
- Support them if they proceed
- Prevent costly mistakes

### Critical Success Factors

1. **Speed** - Policy change = time-sensitive, move fast
2. **Validation** - Test demand before building
3. **Pricing** - Test high prices (£3k-10k), corporate budgets
4. **Value Focus** - Preventive guidance, not just networking
5. **Execution** - Start small, prove model, then scale

### Risks

1. **Timing** - Policy change might be delayed or changed
2. **Market Validation** - Need to prove employers will pay
3. **Competition** - Consultancies might move in quickly
4. **Execution** - Need to deliver real value, not just networking

### Action Plan

**Week 1-2: Research**
- Understand policy change details
- Identify 20 large levy employers
- Research their current levy usage

**Week 3-4: Validation**
- Conduct 10 customer interviews
- Test interest and pricing
- Validate pain points

**Week 5-8: Pilot**
- Run free pilot event
- Get feedback and commitments
- Refine value proposition

**Week 9-12: Launch**
- Get first 10 paying members
- Deliver first event
- Prove value

- Can leverage relationships from preemptive network

## 💰 FINANCIAL COMPARISON

### Preemptive Large Levy Employer Network

**Assumptions:**
- Market: 100+ employers
- Conversion: 10% = 10 members Year 1
- Price: £5,000/year (premium for preventive value)
- Events: 4 per year
- Cost per event: £10,000
- Annual fixed: £10,000
- Your time: £50,000

**Year 1:**
- Revenue: 10 × £5,000 = £50,000
- Costs: (4 × £10,000) + £10,000 + £50,000 = £100,000
- **Loss: -£50,000** (need funding)

**Year 2 (20 members):**
- Revenue: 20 × £5,000 = £100,000
- Costs: £100,000
- **Break-even**

**Year 3 (30 members):**
- Revenue: 30 × £5,000 = £150,000
- Costs: £100,000
- **Profit: £50,000**

**Break-even: 20 members (20% of 100 market)**

### Large Employer Providers Network

**Assumptions:**
- Market: 200 employers
- Conversion: 10% = 20 members Year 1
- Price: £3,000/year
- Events: 4 per year
- Cost per event: £10,000
- Annual fixed: £10,000
- Your time: £50,000

**Year 1:**
- Revenue: 20 × £3,000 = £60,000
- Costs: £100,000
- **Loss: -£40,000**

**Year 2 (40 members):**
- Revenue: 40 × £3,000 = £120,000
- Costs: £100,000
- **Profit: £20,000**

**Break-even: 34 members (17% of 200 market)**

### FE Training Provider Networks

**Assumptions:**
- Market: 1,600 providers
- Conversion: 5% = 80 members Year 1 (across 4 networks)
- Price: £1,000/year per network
- Events: 4 per year per network = 16 total
- Cost per event: £8,000
- Annual fixed: £10,000
- Your time: £50,000

**Year 1:**
- Revenue: 80 × £1,000 = £80,000
- Costs: (16 × £8,000) + £10,000 + £50,000 = £188,000
- **Loss: -£108,000**

**Much harder to break even due to lower price point.**
