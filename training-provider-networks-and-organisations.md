# Training Provider and College Communities of Practice, Networks and Membership Organisations

## UK-Based Organisations

### Further Education & Training Providers

1. **Association of Colleges (AoC)**
   - Website: https://www.aoc.co.uk/
   - Description: National membership organisation representing further education and sixth form colleges in England
   - Focus: Policy, advocacy, professional development

2. **Association of Employment and Learning Providers (AELP)**
   - Website: https://www.aelp.org.uk/
   - Description: National membership body representing independent training providers, colleges and employers
   - Focus: Apprenticeships, skills training, employment

3. **The Education and Training Foundation (ETF)**
   - Website: https://www.et-foundation.co.uk/
   - Description: Professional body for the Further Education and Training sector
   - Focus: Professional development, standards, research

4. **The Skills Network**
   - Website: https://www.theskillsnetwork.com/
   - Description: Online learning provider network
   - Focus: Digital learning, qualifications

5. **The 157 Group**
   - Description: Network of large, influential further education colleges
   - Focus: Leadership, policy influence

6. **Sixth Form Colleges Association (SFCA)**
   - Website: https://www.sixthformcolleges.org/
   - Description: National membership organisation for sixth form colleges
   - Focus: Sixth form education, policy

7. **Landex (Land-Based Colleges Aspiring to Excellence)**
   - Website: https://landex.org.uk/
   - Description: Network of land-based colleges and universities
   - Focus: Agriculture, land management, environmental education

8. **The Mixed Economy Group (MEG)**
   - Description: Network of colleges with commercial trading arms
   - Focus: Commercial activities, enterprise

9. **The Gazelle Colleges Group**
   - Description: Network of colleges focused on entrepreneurship and innovation
   - Focus: Enterprise education, innovation

10. **The 157 Group of Colleges**
    - Description: Network of large, influential further education colleges
    - Focus: Leadership, strategic development

## Notes

- This list includes both membership organisations and informal networks
- Some organisations may have overlapping memberships
- Regional focus varies - many are UK-specific, with some international networks included
- Organisations range from large national bodies to smaller specialist networks
- Some networks may be informal or have limited online presence

## Recommendations for Further Research

1. Check each organisation's website for current membership information
2. Review sector-specific publications and directories
3. Contact sector bodies for comprehensive membership lists
4. Review government education department resources
5. Check professional association directories
6. Review conference and event sponsors/exhibitors in the education sector
