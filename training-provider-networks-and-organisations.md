# Training Provider and College Communities of Practice, Networks and Membership Organisations

## United Kingdom

### Further Education & Training Provider Networks

#### Membership Organisations
1. **Association of Colleges (AoC)**
   - Website: https://www.aoc.co.uk/
   - Description: National membership organisation representing further education colleges in England
   - Focus: Colleges, sixth form colleges, and specialist colleges

2. **Association of Employment and Learning Providers (AELP)**
   - Website: https://www.aelp.org.uk/
   - Description: National membership body representing independent training providers
   - Focus: Apprenticeships, adult education, and skills training

3. **Independent Training Providers' Network (ITPN)**
   - Description: Network for independent training providers
   - Focus: Skills development and vocational training

4. **Federation of Awarding Bodies (FAB)**
   - Website: https://www.awarding.org.uk/
   - Description: Trade association for awarding organisations
   - Focus: Qualifications, assessment, and certification

5. **The Education and Training Foundation (ETF)**
   - Website: https://www.et-foundation.co.uk/
   - Description: Professional body for the further education and training sector
   - Focus: Workforce development and professional standards

6. **Association of Learning Providers (ALP)**
   - Description: Membership organisation for learning providers
   - Focus: Training and skills development

## Notes

- This list includes major national and international networks

- Websites and contact information should be verified for current accuracy
- Many of these organizations offer membership, events, resources, and professional development opportunities

## Research Recommendations

To find additional networks and organizations:
1. Check regional government education departments
2. Search for local training provider associations
3. Look for sector-specific networks (e.g., healthcare training, construction training)
4. Check professional body websites for affiliated networks
5. Review conference and event organizers in the education sector
6. Search for LinkedIn groups and online communities
7. Check education trade publications for organization listings
