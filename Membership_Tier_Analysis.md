# Membership Tier Analysis - Critical Evaluation

## Business Model Overview

**Strategy:** Low entry price + upsell to higher tiers + training course upsells
**Target:** Large levy employers / Employer providers
**Approach:** Freemium/upsell model

---

## 🎯 MEMBERSHIP TIERS

### Tier 1: Entry - £100/month (£1,200/year)

**Includes:**
- 1 full day event each quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 20% off training courses
- 2 half-day training courses per quarter (8 per year) - at 20% discount

**Value Proposition:** Low barrier to entry, basic access

### Tier 2: Member - £280/month (£3,360/year)

**Includes:**
- 2 places (2 people can attend)
- Rising star network (shadow, supported)
- All training included (free)
- 1 full day event per quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 2 online half-days each month (24 per year)

**Value Proposition:** Full access, training included, rising star support

### Tier 3: Growth - £420/month (£5,040/year)

**Includes:**
- 3 places (3 people can attend)
- All Member benefits
- All training free
- 6 online half-days per month (72 per year, "unlimited")
- Additional benefits (TBD)

**Value Proposition:** Premium tier, maximum access

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL ISSUES

### 1. PRICING IS TOO LOW - You're Undervaluing

**The Problem:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**For Large Employers:**
- Corporate budgets = they can pay MORE
- £1,200/year is "cheap" = may signal low value
- You're leaving money on the table
- Competitors may charge 2-3x more

**Reality Check:**
- Large employers pay £5,000-10,000+ for similar networks
- Your "premium" tier is £5,040 = mid-range, not premium
- Entry at £1,200 = too cheap, may attract wrong customers

**Recommendation:**
- Entry: £200-300/month (£2,400-3,600/year)
- Member: £500-700/month (£6,000-8,400/year)
- Growth: £800-1,200/month (£9,600-14,400/year)

**Why:** Corporate budgets can handle this. Higher price = higher perceived value.

---

### 2. "CHEAP ENTRY + UPSELL" STRATEGY RISKS

**The Problem:**
- Low entry price attracts price-sensitive customers
- They may not upsell (they chose cheap for a reason)
- Low lifetime value
- High churn risk

**Reality:**
- People who buy "cheap" often stay cheap
- Upsell conversion is typically 10-20% (not guaranteed)
- You need 5-10x entry customers to match one premium customer
- More customers = more support = higher costs

**Better Strategy:**
- Start higher, offer value
- Don't rely on upsell
- Each tier should be profitable on its own
- Upsell is bonus, not core model

---

### 3. TRAINING COURSE UPSELL - COMPLEXITY RISK

**The Problem:**
- You're giving away training in higher tiers
- Then trying to upsell training courses
- Contradictory value proposition
- Confusing for customers

**Example:**
- Member tier: "All training included (free)"
- But then: "Upsell training courses!"
- **Question:** What training is included? What's upsell?

**Issues:**
1. **Value Confusion:**
   - If training is "free" in Member/Growth, why upsell?
   - What's the difference?
   - Customers will be confused

2. **Cannibalization:**
   - Free training reduces upsell opportunity
   - Why would they pay for training if it's "free"?
   - Contradictory model

3. **Complexity:**
   - Hard to explain
   - Hard to sell
   - Hard to deliver

**Recommendation:**
- **Option A:** Training included = specific courses, upsell = additional/advanced
- **Option B:** Training always separate, membership = discount
- **Option C:** Training included = basic, upsell = premium/advanced

**Clarify:** What training is included? What's upsell? Make it clear.

---

### 4. COST STRUCTURE - CAN YOU DELIVER?

**The Math Problem:**

**Entry Tier (£100/month):**
- 4 full-day events per year
- 12 monthly meetups
- 8 half-day training courses (at 20% discount)
- **Cost per event:** Venue, catering, your time, speakers
- **Estimated cost:** £5,000-10,000 per full-day event
- **Annual cost:** £20,000-40,000 for events alone
- **Break-even:** Need 17-33 members just for events
- **Plus:** Training courses, meetups, support
- **Reality:** You need 30-50 members to break even

**Member Tier (£280/month):**
- 2 places = 2x the people
- All training free = higher costs
- 24 online half-days = more delivery
- **Cost:** Higher per member
- **Break-even:** Need 15-25 members

**Growth Tier (£420/month):**
- 3 places = 3x the people
- 72 online half-days = massive delivery
- "Unlimited" = cost risk
- **Cost:** Highest per member
- **Break-even:** Need 12-20 members

**Question:** Can you deliver this at these prices? Do the math.

---

### 5. "RISING STAR NETWORK" - UNCLARITY

**The Problem:**
- "Rising star network (shadow, supported)"
- What does this mean?
- How do you deliver it?
- What's the value?

**Issues:**
1. **Unclear Value:**
   - What is "shadow"?
   - What is "supported"?
   - How is this different from regular membership?

2. **Delivery Complexity:**
   - Requires your time
   - One-on-one support?
   - Group support?
   - How scalable?

3. **Pricing:**
   - Is this worth the extra £180/month?
   - Can you deliver it?
   - What's the cost?

**Recommendation:**
- Clarify what "rising star network" means
- Define the value
- Ensure you can deliver
- Price accordingly

---

### 6. "UNLIMITED" TRAINING - COST RISK

**The Problem:**
- Growth tier: "6 online half-days per month (unlimited)"
- "Unlimited" = unlimited cost risk
- What if someone uses it heavily?
   - 10 half-days per month?
   - 20 half-days per month?
   - Your cost goes up, revenue stays same

**Reality:**
- "Unlimited" attracts heavy users
- They cost more to serve
- You can't scale
- Profitability risk

**Recommendation:**
- Cap it: "Up to 6 per month" or "72 per year"
- Or: "Unlimited" but with fair use policy
- Or: Remove "unlimited" language
- Protect yourself from cost overruns

---

### 7. VALUE PROPOSITION - NOT CLEAR ENOUGH

**The Problem:**
- What's the core value?
- Why would someone pay £100 vs £280 vs £420?
- What problem are you solving?
- How is this different from competitors?

**Current Value Props:**
- Entry: "Basic access, some training"
- Member: "Full access, all training, rising star"
- Growth: "Everything, unlimited"

**Issues:**
- Not compelling enough
- Doesn't address pain points
- Generic benefits
- No clear differentiation

**Better Value Props:**
- Entry: "Understand Growth and Skills Levy, make informed decisions"
- Member: "Strategic guidance + operational support, avoid costly mistakes"
- Growth: "Full support + unlimited access, maximize levy value"

**Focus on:** Outcomes, not features.

---

## 🟡 MODERATE CONCERNS

### 8. MONTHLY MEETUPS - DELIVERY CHALLENGE

**The Problem:**
- 12 monthly meetups per year
- For all tiers
- Requires consistent delivery
- What if you can't deliver one month?

**Issues:**
- Consistency required
- Content needs to be fresh
- Attendance may vary
- Delivery burden

**Recommendation:**
- Start with quarterly, not monthly
- Or: Virtual monthly, in-person quarterly
- Or: Make it optional/on-demand
- Reduce delivery burden

---

### 9. TRAINING COURSE PRICING - UNCLEAR

**The Problem:**
- Entry: 20% off training
- Member/Growth: Training "free"
- But: Upsell training courses
- **Question:** What's the training price? What's the upsell?

**Issues:**
1. **Pricing Unclear:**
   - What does training cost normally?
   - What's 20% off of?
   - What's the upsell price?

2. **Value Unclear:**
   - If training is "free" in Member, what's upsell?
   - Premium training?
   - Additional training?
   - Unclear

**Recommendation:**
- Define training pricing clearly
- Clarify what's included vs. upsell
- Make it simple to understand

---

### 10. SCALABILITY CONCERNS

**The Problem:**
- Full-day events = expensive (venue, catering)
- Monthly meetups = time-intensive
- Training delivery = your time
- "Unlimited" = cost risk
- How do you scale?

**Issues:**
- More members = more events needed
- More members = more support
- More members = higher costs
- Can you scale profitably?

**Recommendation:**
- Start small, prove model
- Virtual events = lower cost
- Limit "unlimited" features
- Plan for scalability
- Consider outsourcing

- Member: £280/month

**Recommended:**
- Entry: £250/month (£3,000/year)
- Member: £600/month (£7,200/year)

**Why:**
- Corporate budgets can handle this
- Higher perceived value
- More sustainable
- Better margins

- Membership = access to network, events
- Training = separate pricing

- Clear distinction

- Included: Basic/standard training
- Upsell: Advanced/premium/one-on-one training
- Clear value ladder

**Recommendation:** Option C - Clear, simple, scalable

- Clearer value props
- Simpler to explain

- More sustainable

### 4. Remove "Unlimited" Language

**Problem:** "Unlimited" = cost risk

**Solution:**
- "Up to 6 per month" or "72 per year"
- Or: "Unlimited" with fair use policy

### 5. Clarify "Rising Star Network"

**Current:** "Rising star network (shadow, supported)"

- Ensure you can deliver
- Price accordingly

- Reduce delivery burden

## 💰 FINANCIAL MODEL

### Assumptions:

**Costs per Full-Day Event:**
- Venue: £3,000-5,000
- Catering: £2,000-3,000
- AV/Production: £1,000-2,000
- Speaker fees: £2,000-5,000
- Your time: £1,000-2,000
- **Total: £9,000-17,000 per event**

**Costs per Training Course:**
- Your time: £500-1,000
- Materials: £100-200
- Platform: £50-100
- **Total: £650-1,300 per course**

**Annual Costs:**
- 4 full-day events: £36,000-68,000
- 12 training courses (Member tier): £7,800-15,600
- 4 quarterly meetups: £4,000-8,000
- Platform/tech: £5,000-10,000
- Marketing: £10,000-20,000
- Your salary: £50,000-100,000
- **Total: £112,800-221,600**

### Break-Even Analysis:

**Current Pricing:**
- Entry: £100/month = £1,200/year
- Member: £280/month = £3,360/year
- Growth: £420/month = £5,040/year

**Break-Even:**
- Need 19-37 Entry members, OR
- Need 7-14 Member members, OR
- Need 5-10 Growth members
- **Or mix:** 10 Entry + 5 Member + 3 Growth = £12,000 + £16,800 + £15,120 = £43,920

**Reality:** You need 30-50 total members to break even at current pricing.

**Recommended Pricing:**
- Entry: £250/month = £3,000/year
- Member: £600/month = £7,200/year
- Growth: £1,000/month = £12,000/year

**Break-Even:**
- Need 8-15 Entry members, OR
- Need 3-6 Member members, OR
- Need 2-4 Growth members
- **Or mix:** 5 Entry + 3 Member + 2 Growth = £15,000 + £21,600 + £24,000 = £60,600

**Reality:** You need 10-15 total members to break even at recommended pricing.
