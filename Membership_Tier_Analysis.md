# Membership Tier Analysis: Low Entry + Upsell Strategy

## Business Model Overview

**Strategy:** Low entry price to attract members, then upsell to higher tiers and training courses.

**Three Tiers:**
1. **Entry:** £100/month - Basic access
2. **Member:** £280/month - Enhanced access
3. **Growth:** £420/month - Premium access

**Upsell Strategy:** Training courses as additional revenue

---

## 🎯 TIER BREAKDOWN

### Tier 1: Entry - £100/month (£1,200/year)

**What's Included:**
- 1 full day event each quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 20% off training courses
- Training: 2 half days per quarter (8 per year) - but only 20% off

**Value Proposition:**
- Low barrier to entry
- Basic networking
- Discount on training

**Target:** Price-sensitive members, testing the water

---

### Tier 2: Member - £280/month (£3,360/year)

**What's Included:**
- 2 places (can bring colleague/team member)
- Rising star network (shadow, supported)
- All training included (FREE)
- 1 full day event per quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 2 online half days each month (24 per year)

**Value Proposition:**
- Team access (2 places)
- All training free (vs. 20% off)
- More training (24 online half days vs. 8 with discount)
- Rising star network (mentoring/shadowing)

**Target:** Committed members, teams, those who want training

---

### Tier 3: Growth - £420/month (£5,040/year)

**What's Included:**
- 3 places (can bring 2 colleagues/team members)
- All Member benefits
- All training free
- 1 full day event per quarter (4 per year)
- Monthly meetups (12 per year)
- 6 online half days per month (unlimited) - 72+ per year

**Value Proposition:**
- Team access (3 places)
- Unlimited training (6+ online half days per month)
- Premium access
- All benefits

**Target:** Large teams, heavy training users, premium clients

## 🎯 STRATEGY EVALUATION: "Cheap Entry + Upsell"

### ✅ Advantages

1. **Low Barrier to Entry:**
   - £100/month = easy decision
   - Low risk for prospects
   - Easy to say "yes"

2. **Upsell Opportunity:**
   - Members see value, upgrade
   - Training courses = additional revenue
   - Natural progression

3. **Volume Play:**
   - Get more members at entry level
   - Some will upgrade
   - Some will buy training

4. **Price Anchoring:**
   - Entry seems cheap vs. Member/Growth
   - Makes higher tiers look valuable
   - Psychological pricing

### ⚠️ Risks

1. **Low Revenue per Member:**
   - Entry tier = £1,200/year
   - Need many members to break even
   - High volume required

2. **Upsell Not Guaranteed:**
   - Members may stay at Entry level
   - May not buy training
   - Low lifetime value

3. **Cost Structure:**
   - Full day events are expensive
   - Monthly meetups add up
   - Training costs (if included)
   - May not be profitable at Entry level

4. **Value Perception:**
   - Entry may seem "cheap" = low value
   - Hard to justify higher tiers
   - May attract wrong customers

## 🔍 CRITICAL ISSUES

### Issue 1: Entry Tier May Not Be Profitable

**Problem:**
- £100/month = £1,200/year
- But: 4 full day events + 12 monthly meetups = significant costs
- If costs are £500+ per member, margin is thin
- Need high volume to make money

**Question:** Can you deliver Entry tier profitably at £100/month?

**Solution Options:**
1. Increase Entry price to £150-200/month
2. Reduce Entry benefits (fewer events)
3. Make Entry virtual-only (lower costs)
4. Accept Entry as loss leader (upsell required)

### Issue 2: Training Costs Unclear

**Problem:**
- Member/Growth tiers include "all training free"
- But: Training costs money (consultants, content, delivery)
- If training is expensive, you lose money
- Need to understand training economics

**Questions:**
- What does training cost you?
- How much training will members use?
- Can you deliver profitably?

**Solution Options:**
1. Limit training (e.g., 2 per quarter, not unlimited)
2. Use lower-cost training (recorded, not live consultants)
3. Charge for premium training (upsell)
4. Make training self-service (lower costs)

### Issue 3: "Unlimited" Training Risk

**Problem:**
- Growth tier: "6 online half days per month (unlimited)"
- Risk: Heavy users cost you money
- If training costs £2,000 per half day, 6 = £12,000/month
- But member pays £420/month = you lose £11,580/month!

**Question:** How do you cap costs while offering "unlimited"?

**Solution Options:**
1. Remove "unlimited" - cap at 6 per month
2. Use recorded/self-service training (lower costs)
3. Charge for premium/live training (upsell)
4. Limit to specific training catalog (not all training)

### Issue 4: Upsell Training Courses

**Problem:**
- Entry tier gets 20% off training
- But: If training is expensive, 20% off may not be enough incentive
- Need to understand training pricing

**Questions:**
- How much do training courses cost?
- What's the margin?
- Will Entry members buy at 20% off?

**Solution Options:**
1. Make training courses attractive (good value)
2. Higher discount for Entry (30-40% off)
3. Bundle training with membership upgrade
4. Create training specifically for upsell
